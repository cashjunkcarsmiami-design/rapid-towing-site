Clarifying Key Drop-Off Instructions Before a Hollywood Tow

October 22, 2026

A shop that can receive a vehicle may still have specific instructions for the keys. Before arranging delivery in Hollywood, ask how that handoff should work for your appointment. Do not assume a visible drop box can be used without the shop's confirmation.

Get instructions from the receiving business

Confirm the correct entrance, permitted parking position and key procedure for the expected arrival time. Ask whether the shop needs identifying information attached to the keys and what can be shared safely. Do not include unnecessary personal documents or passwords. If the instructions are unclear, resolve them before authorizing delivery.

Communicate the plan to dispatch

Pass along the confirmed instructions and the shop contact who provided them. If the location closes early or changes its process, notify dispatch and ask whether a new arrangement is needed. A tow request does not guarantee that an unconfirmed shop will accept the vehicle. Keep pickup authorization, repair intake and payment arrangements clearly distinguished.
