Ask about the actual payment arrangement

Confirm which payment methods the company accepts for your request and when payment is expected. Do not infer those details from another customer's experience. If somebody else will pay, make that part of the conversation before service is authorized.

Distinguish a quote from coverage

If you plan to seek reimbursement through another provider, confirm that process with that provider separately. Do not assume a towing company can guarantee coverage or that a reference number means payment has been approved. Keep those questions separate from the operator's service quote.

Review changes before accepting them

Ask what the quoted service includes and how any proposed additional work would be discussed. Request an explanation if the scope changes. Use the company's agreed payment channel and obtain the service record; avoid sharing payment credentials in public posts or unrelated group messages.
