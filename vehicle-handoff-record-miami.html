Keeping a Simple Vehicle Handoff Record in Miami

September 28, 2026

A short handoff record can help you and the receiving shop refer to the same vehicle and instructions. It does not need to be complicated: focus on what was agreed and keep transport information separate from repair questions.

Record the agreed basics

Keep the vehicle description, pickup address, destination and dispatch contact together. Note who will release the vehicle and who expects it at the destination. If you discuss an adjustment by phone, write down the updated arrangement rather than leaving conflicting versions in separate messages.

Describe observations accurately

If you already have photos taken safely, keep them with your records. Label known concerns in plain language without presenting a guess as a mechanical diagnosis. Do not enter an unsafe area or move a disabled vehicle just to complete a photo checklist.

Confirm the handoff information

Ask what pickup or delivery confirmation the company provides and keep the service receipt. Tell the receiving shop about any agreed key handoff or instructions. Delivery confirmation means the transport step is complete; it does not mean the shop has inspected, accepted a repair estimate or finished work on the vehicle.

Resumen en español

Guarda juntos los datos de recogida, destino y contactos. Describe lo que sabes del carro y conserva la confirmación del traslado; eso no equivale a un diagnóstico del taller.
