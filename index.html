Information that helps dispatch

What to Tell Us When You Need a Tow

Clear information helps us understand the situation and discuss the right service. When you call, provide your location, vehicle details, the problem you are experiencing, and where the vehicle needs to go. Mention any access or safety issue before assistance is arranged.

Your Exact Location

Share the street, nearest intersection, business name, highway direction, exit number, or a location pin. Explain whether the vehicle is in traffic, a parking garage, a gated property, or another restricted area.

Vehicle Information

Provide the year, make, model, color, and vehicle type. Tell us if it is an SUV, truck, lowered vehicle, all-wheel-drive vehicle, or has modifications that may affect loading.

Current Condition

Explain whether the car starts, rolls, steers, has flat tires, is damaged, is missing a wheel, or is stuck. Photos can help when the situation is difficult to describe.

Requested Destination

Have the repair shop, home, dealership, or other destination ready. Confirm that the destination can receive the vehicle, especially outside normal business hours.

Your safety comes first

What to Do While Waiting for Roadside Help

If the vehicle can be moved safely, get as far away from active traffic as possible, turn on the hazard lights, and stay aware of surrounding vehicles. Do not stand between cars or attempt repairs in an unsafe lane. If there is a collision, injury, fire, fuel leak, or immediate danger, contact emergency services first.

Keep your phone available and watch for calls or messages about arrival details. If your location changes or another company assists you, let the dispatcher know. Never leave keys or personal information in an unattended vehicle unless clear arrangements have been made.

The right equipment for the situation

Choosing Towing or Roadside Assistance

Flatbed Transport

A flatbed may be appropriate for vehicles with significant damage, low clearance, some all-wheel-drive systems, specialty vehicles, or cars that should be transported with all wheels off the road.

Jump Start

If the main problem appears to be a weak battery, describe what happens when you turn the key or press the start button. A vehicle may still need towing if it does not remain running.

Lockout Help

Confirm the vehicle location, make and model, and whether a child, pet, or medical concern is involved. For any immediate danger, contact emergency services.

Accident Recovery

Explain visible damage, wheel condition, fluid leaks, and whether police or property management are controlling the scene. Do not move a vehicle when authorities have instructed otherwise.

Details for difficult locations

Garages, Gated Properties, and Difficult Pickups

Parking garages may have height limits and narrow turns. Gated communities, private lots, airports, and commercial properties may require authorization before entry. Tell us about clearance limits, access codes, property rules, locked gates, or vehicles blocked by other cars.

For a smoother service, remove important belongings, keep the keys available, and confirm the destination address. Pricing and arrival timing can depend on distance, traffic, vehicle condition, location access, and the equipment required, so provide complete information when requesting service.
