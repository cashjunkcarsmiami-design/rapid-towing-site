Preparing for Towing From Hotel or Guest Parking in Miami Springs

A disabled vehicle in hotel or guest parking may require property authorization and clear directions. Confirm the location before dispatch so the driver does not arrive at the wrong entrance or an inaccessible level.

Confirm Property Permission

Tell the front desk, host or property manager that the vehicle needs assistance. Ask whether commercial vehicles use a particular entrance and whether a gate code, room number or parking authorization is required.

Describe the Exact Space

Provide the property name, address, parking level, space number and nearest visible landmark. Mention low clearance, tight turns, valet areas or other vehicles blocking access.

Explain the Vehicle Condition

Share the year, make, model, problem, key status and whether the vehicle starts, rolls and steers. Explain whether roadside assistance might solve the problem or the vehicle must be transported.

Confirm the Destination

Have the repair facility or destination address ready and verify that it can receive the vehicle. Remove personal belongings and stay available for arrival instructions.
