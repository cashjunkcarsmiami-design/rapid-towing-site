MIAMI TOWING GUIDE

Towing a Long-Parked Car in Hialeah or Miami

October 14, 2026

A vehicle that has been parked for months needs a description of its condition today, not simply how it drove before storage. Before arranging a tow from a Hialeah driveway or Miami property, gather information you can observe safely and tell dispatch what remains unknown. The purpose is to plan transport, not to diagnose or repair the vehicle by phone.

A long storage period does not establish a single mechanical problem. The car may have a battery issue, flat tires, missing parts or access restrictions, but do not assume any of those conditions without checking what you actually know.

Use current observations

Provide the year, make, model and keys available. Explain when the car was last used and whether it has been inspected since. Keep an older repair report separate from a description of its present condition. If it has not been started recently, say that rather than describing it as running.

Tell dispatch about visible tire condition, missing wheels, damage or removed components. Report whether movement is known or untested. You do not need to attempt starting, pushing or repairing the car solely to make an initial towing inquiry.

Photograph the route to the vehicle

Current photographs can show the car’s position and the surrounding space. Include the entrance, driveway or parking area where useful. Explain any view you cannot reach safely. A photograph from before the car was stored may not show a gate, vegetation or another vehicle now blocking access.

For a Hialeah residential pickup, identify who can open the property and whether a shared driveway requires coordination. If the vehicle is behind equipment or other parked cars, discuss the obstacle before scheduling. Let the operator review access rather than promising that a truck can reach it.

Separate towing from repair preparation

A tow moves the vehicle to an agreed destination; it does not establish that the car is safe to drive after arrival. If your goal is to return it to service, arrange the receiving inspection with the repair facility. Ask the shop what information it needs and whether it can accept a car that may not roll or steer normally.

Do not assume that a jump start will make a long-parked vehicle ready for the road. Describe the current condition and discuss whether the requested service is roadside assistance, vehicle transport or another assessment.

Prepare belongings, keys and authorization

Remove accessible personal items you intend to keep and explain any compartment you cannot open. Separate loose belongings from vehicle components that should travel with the car. Confirm who can authorize transport and who will be available at pickup.

If the owner will be away, give dispatch the agreed on-site contact. Keep keys and receiving instructions organized without assuming that any person at the property can approve every change to the transport request.

Confirm the transport plan

Before booking, confirm the pickup point, destination, quoted scope, equipment assessment and collection window. Arrange receiving access at the repair shop or other destination. If the car is moved, a part is removed or driveway access changes after the first conversation, update dispatch before the truck arrives.

For additional preparation, review our vehicle towing checklist and Hialeah towing information.

Resumen en español

Para trasladar un carro que lleva meses estacionado, describe su condición actual y no solamente cómo funcionaba antes. Avisa si tienes las llaves, cómo están las ruedas y qué bloquea el acceso. No hagas reparaciones ni movimientos peligrosos para pedir una grúa. Confirma el destino, quién recibirá el carro, el precio y la recogida.
