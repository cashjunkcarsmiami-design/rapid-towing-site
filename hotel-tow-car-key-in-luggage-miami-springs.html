Vehicle Keys Packed in Luggage Before a Hotel Tow

Rapid Towing · October 25, 2026

During a hotel departure in Miami Springs, vehicle keys can be packed into luggage that leaves separately. Before coordinating a tow, confirm where the keys actually are and who can provide them. Saying you have the keys is incomplete if they are no longer at the pickup location.

Separate luggage movement from vehicle handoff

Check with the person who has the bag and establish whether the keys can be made available under an agreed plan. Do not assume a hotel or driver will accept responsibility for retrieving them. Avoid sharing access to personal accounts or baggage locations publicly to resolve the situation. Dispatch only needs the practical facts relevant to the vehicle.

Explain the limitation before service is finalized

Tell dispatch whether the keys are available, expected later or currently inaccessible. Do not promise an immediate handoff based on an unconfirmed delivery. Ask how the situation affects the service assessment and coordination. If the keys arrive or their location changes, update dispatch promptly and confirm which person will meet the operator rather than leaving an earlier plan in place.
