Forklift Traffic Near a Vehicle Awaiting a Medley Tow

Rapid Towing · October 26, 2026

A disabled vehicle inside a Medley work yard may sit near an active forklift route. Mention that activity when requesting towing and involve the person responsible for the yard. A location that is easy to identify may still require coordination with the work happening around it.

Describe the activity from a safe position

Explain where the vehicle is relative to the yard entrance and the operating area. Do not step into an active route to take a better photo or attempt to direct industrial equipment yourself. Ask the site's responsible contact what access arrangements can be made for the proposed pickup.

Coordinate before treating the route as clear

Pass confirmed access information to dispatch and identify the yard contact who can provide updates. If operations change, communicate the change before arrival. Avoid promising that equipment will stop at a particular time unless the responsible person has confirmed it. The tow should be planned around actual conditions rather than a guessed quiet period.
