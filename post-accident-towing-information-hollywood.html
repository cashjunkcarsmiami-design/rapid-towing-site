Information to Prepare After an Accident Tow in Hollywood

Safety and official instructions come first after a collision. Once emergency needs are addressed, clear information helps organize a towing request.

Prioritize Safety

Call emergency services for injuries, fire, leaking fuel or an immediate traffic hazard. Follow police or road-authority instructions and do not stand in active traffic.

Share the Exact Location

Provide the road, direction of travel, nearest intersection and a safe callback number. Explain whether authorities or another service are already present.

Describe Visible Damage

Report deployed airbags, damaged wheels, leaking fluids, steering or suspension damage and whether the vehicle can roll. Do not attempt to drive a vehicle that may be unsafe.

Choose and Confirm the Destination

Ask the repair shop, storage location or other authorized destination whether it can receive the vehicle. Keep insurance and incident information for the appropriate parties.
