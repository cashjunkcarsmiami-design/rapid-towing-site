Planning a Vehicle Pickup Across a Shift Change in Medley

September 28, 2026

A vehicle pickup at a business can span two employee shifts. The next person on duty needs more than the message that a truck might arrive: they need the agreed vehicle and contact details.

Prepare one handover note

Record the vehicle description, its location within the property and the dispatch callback number. Include the destination and any confirmed access instructions. Keep tentative arrival estimates clearly labeled so the next shift does not mistake an estimate for a guaranteed appointment.

Confirm the incoming contact

Make sure the next employee knows about the request and can handle the agreed release process. Tell dispatch who will answer after the shift change. Do not assume a shared switchboard or security desk has automatically received the instructions.

Check what still needs approval

Identify missing keys, pending authorization or access questions before the outgoing contact leaves. Share sensitive entry information only through the agreed channel. If a required person becomes unavailable, update dispatch and discuss the plan rather than leaving the driver to discover the issue at the gate.
