Moving a Child Seat to a Separate Ride Before a Tow

Rapid Towing · October 24, 2026

When a family arranges separate transportation during a tow in Miami Gardens, a child seat may still be in the disabled vehicle. Think about that need while coordinating the family's ride. Do not assume the tow vehicle can carry passengers or provide an appropriate seat.

Plan the separate ride first

Tell the person organizing transportation what seating is needed and follow the seat and vehicle manufacturers' instructions for its use. Only retrieve items when the location is safe; do not enter traffic or an unsafe vehicle to speed up the plan. If you cannot access the seat safely, explain the situation to the relevant transportation provider and arrange an appropriate alternative.

Keep dispatch informed about the vehicle handoff

Let dispatch know if the person with the keys needs to leave with the family or if another authorized contact will remain. Confirm who will receive updates and meet the operator. Passenger arrangements and vehicle transport are different parts of the situation. Resolve both explicitly so a towing request does not depend on an assumption about where the family will wait or travel.
