A Fleet Vehicle Still Booked on the Company's Calendar

Rapid Towing · October 25, 2026

A business vehicle may be scheduled for towing while another employee still expects to use it. Before confirming a planned tow in Doral, resolve that conflict with the person responsible for the fleet. Dispatch should receive a realistic availability plan rather than two incompatible instructions.

Check the internal handoff

Identify when the vehicle will stop being used and who will have the keys. Distinguish a desired pickup time from the time the business can actually make the vehicle available. Do not ask an employee to operate a vehicle whose condition is uncertain simply to keep the calendar unchanged. Report its known condition accurately when requesting the service.

Update dispatch before confirming

Once the internal plan is settled, provide the current location, destination and authorized contact. Explain if the vehicle's condition or contents changed during its final use. Ask whether the estimate or equipment assessment needs review. The business's internal booking does not reserve a tow, and a towing discussion does not remove the vehicle from the company's schedule; both arrangements need explicit coordination.
