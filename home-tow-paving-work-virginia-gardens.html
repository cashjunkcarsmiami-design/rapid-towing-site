Fresh Driveway Work Before a Virginia Gardens Vehicle Pickup

Rapid Towing · October 26, 2026

If driveway work has taken place at a Virginia Gardens pickup address, tell dispatch before a tow is arranged. A driveway that was usable in an earlier photo may now have restricted access. The contractor or property contact should clarify any limits related to the work.

Explain what changed

Describe the affected area and whether the car is on one side of it or beyond it. Do not assume that a surface is ready for vehicle traffic because it looks finished. Share the property's confirmed access instructions rather than inventing a waiting period or a weight limit for the new surface.

Revisit the pickup plan

Ask dispatch to assess the situation with the current information. If access is not ready, discuss the next step before arranging a truck. Keep the property contact involved when conditions change. A short update about the driveway can prevent an arrival based on a route that no longer reaches the vehicle as previously described.
