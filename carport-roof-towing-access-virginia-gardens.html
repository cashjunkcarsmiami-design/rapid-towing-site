Describing Carport Clearance Before a Virginia Gardens Tow

October 22, 2026

A car parked under a carport in Virginia Gardens may need additional access review before towing. The open driveway does not show every restriction around the vehicle. Tell dispatch where the roof ends, whether columns are close and whether the vehicle can move independently.

Use a wide view of the current position

If you can do so safely, photograph the vehicle together with the roof supports and the approach. State whether any measurements are confirmed or only estimates. Mention fixed objects that cannot be moved. Do not climb the structure, push the car or remove supports to prepare for the request; describe the setup as it exists.

Confirm the plan before changing anything

Ask what additional information is needed for equipment selection. Explain whether another vehicle or stored items block the exit and who can clear ordinary loose objects. If a qualified person later changes the vehicle's position, update dispatch. The estimate and pickup arrangement should reflect the access actually available when service is authorized.
