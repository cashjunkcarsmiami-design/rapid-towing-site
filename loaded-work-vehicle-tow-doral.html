Planning a Tow for a Loaded Work Vehicle in Doral

October 19, 2026

A company van may look like a standard passenger vehicle from outside while carrying tools, inventory or fitted equipment. For a Doral pickup, describe the vehicle as it is currently loaded. The empty vehicle specification alone may not describe the transport request.

List what remains in the vehicle

Give dispatch the make, model and any known weight information, then describe the cargo without guessing a precise total. Mention racks, enclosed equipment and items that could shift. Ask whether the load must be removed or secured by your team before the pickup can be accepted.

Coordinate the work interruption

Choose a company contact who can answer cargo and destination questions. Confirm whether the receiving shop can accept the loaded vehicle and whether valuable tools should stay with your staff. Do not assume the tow truck can separately carry loose inventory or provide cargo storage.
