Preparing an Electric Vehicle for Towing in Doral

Electric vehicles can have drivetrain and high-voltage considerations that affect loading. Identify the exact model and condition before equipment is dispatched.

Give the exact model and symptoms

Share battery state of charge, warning messages, collision or flood exposure and whether the vehicle powers on. Mention locked wheels, low clearance and the location of the charging cable.

Follow manufacturer transport guidance

Some vehicles require flatbed transport or a specific tow mode. Consult the owner’s manual and qualified towing provider. Do not drag locked drive wheels or improvise with unfamiliar release points.

Respect high-voltage hazards

Stay away from exposed orange cables, damaged battery areas, smoke, unusual heat or hissing. Call emergency services for fire or immediate danger and tell dispatch about the hazard.
