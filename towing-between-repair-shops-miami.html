Towing in Miami: Moving a Car Between Repair Shops

Rapid Towing · November 2, 2026

Leer en español

Changing repair shops takes more than choosing a new address. Coordinate the release, vehicle condition and receiving appointment before requesting towing in Miami.

Confirm the first shop can release the vehicle

Speak with the shop currently holding the car before arranging collection. Ask who handles release, when that person is available and whether any account or paperwork questions still need resolving. A towing appointment does not settle a disagreement with a repair business. Tell Rapid Towing who is authorized to arrange the move and who will hand over the car. Keep those contacts distinct if they are different people.

Describe the car as it is after the inspection

A car may be in a different condition from when it entered the first shop. Ask whether it is assembled, where its keys are and whether removed components or loose parts need separate arrangements. Report known wheel, steering or power limitations without asking anyone to improvise a movement procedure. Dispatch needs the current configuration to assess equipment; an old photograph or the original breakdown description may leave out important changes.

Get acceptance from the receiving shop

Give the second shop the vehicle details and ask it to confirm that it will accept delivery. Identify the receiving contact, intake hours and permitted unloading location. A booking for a later repair date does not necessarily mean the shop can store the car today. If the destination has several locations, confirm the exact branch. Share the acknowledged destination with dispatch before agreeing to the transport.

Keep both ends of the Miami trip clear

State the full pickup and delivery addresses separately, including suite or yard information when relevant. A customer entrance can differ from the service entrance a tow truck needs. Explain gates, parked vehicles or other access restrictions at both properties. Ask the responsible shop contacts to provide the approved route rather than assuming a map pin identifies it. Traffic and access can affect the proposed arrival window.

Compare a complete transport quote

For a Miami towing quote, give the year, make, model, actual condition, both locations and the release window. Ask what equipment and service the quoted amount includes, what payment arrangement applies and whether waiting or changed instructions would require a revised estimate. Confirm the actual assignment and availability before treating the move as booked. Do not assume an earlier quote to your home applies to a shop-to-shop trip.

Use a short handoff checklist

Before collection, confirm these five points with the relevant contacts: vehicle ready for release, keys accounted for, current condition reported, receiving shop expecting the car and destination acknowledged by dispatch. Arrange how you will receive the handoff update. Ask before placing loose components inside the vehicle. Call Rapid Towing at 305-457-0817 to review the specific request; the operator determines the loading and unloading method.

What if the second shop changes its intake window?

If the receiving Miami shop postpones intake, contact dispatch before replacing the destination in a message to the driver. Confirm whether the first shop can keep the car until the revised window, and ask whether the transport quote or assignment needs to change. Record the new receiving contact and address together. Do not assume that a truck can wait at a closed workshop or leave the car outside without an accepted unloading arrangement.

Tell dispatch if the first shop has left the car partly dismantled; the receiving appointment alone does not establish that it is ready to load. Confirm which contact will report any change before collection. Do not move heavy parts into the cabin yourself to make the request look complete.
