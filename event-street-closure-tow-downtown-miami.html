Planning a Downtown Miami Tow Around a Street Closure

October 19, 2026

A temporary street closure or a building event can make the usual pickup entrance unavailable. If you are arranging a Downtown Miami tow, describe the restriction you can see or that building management has confirmed. Do not assume yesterday’s access directions still apply.

Distinguish vehicle location from access

Give the vehicle’s exact location and identify the entrance affected by the closure. If management provides a permitted alternate entrance, pass those instructions to dispatch. Avoid inventing a route through restricted lanes or promising that a truck can enter a pedestrian-only area.

Coordinate a practical pickup window

Ask the property contact whether access will change during the expected pickup period. Keep dispatch informed if the closure expands or the building changes instructions. Let the towing operator assess truck access and loading space; moving the disabled vehicle is not automatically a safe workaround.
