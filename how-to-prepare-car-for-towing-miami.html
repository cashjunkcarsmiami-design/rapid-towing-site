How to Prepare Your Car for Towing in Miami

A few minutes of preparation can help a towing appointment go more smoothly. Confirm your location and destination, remove important belongings, find the keys, and explain any damage or access problem before the truck arrives.

Start With Safety

If the vehicle can move safely, pull away from active traffic and turn on the hazard lights. Do not stand between vehicles or attempt repairs near fast-moving traffic. After a collision or in a dangerous road position, follow instructions from emergency responders and dispatch.

Share a location pin, nearest cross street, business name or mile marker. On a highway, confirm the direction of travel and the nearest exit. In a garage or gated property, explain height limits, gate codes and where the truck can enter.

Vehicle Towing Preparation Checklist

  • Remove valuables, documents, medication and electronic devices.
  • Take out loose items that could shift during transport.
  • Locate the ignition key or remote and disclose a missing key.
  • Confirm whether the vehicle starts, rolls, steers and shifts to neutral.
  • Describe flat tires, locked wheels, missing parts or accident damage.
  • Close windows and secure doors when it is safe to do so.
  • Photograph the vehicle and visible damage for your records.
  • Confirm the repair shop, home, storage lot or other destination.

Tell Dispatch About Special Conditions

A car in an open parking lot is different from one inside a low garage, behind another vehicle or missing wheels. Tell Rapid Towing about low clearance, locked steering, a damaged suspension, electric-vehicle requirements, loading restrictions or a vehicle that cannot roll. Photos can help the driver understand the space before arriving.

Do not attempt to release a parking brake, crawl under a damaged vehicle or move broken parts if doing so is unsafe. The towing method and equipment should be selected based on the vehicle's condition and location.

Confirm the Destination Before Pickup

Call the repair shop or destination first when possible. Confirm its address, business hours, after-hours drop instructions and whether it will accept the vehicle. If the tow is related to an accident or insurance claim, keep the claim and contact information available.

Ask for any available service details before authorizing the tow. Keep a record of the company name, phone number, vehicle destination and relevant instructions.

Car Towing Preparation FAQ

How do I prepare my car for towing?

Remove valuables, locate the keys, confirm the destination and tell dispatch about damage, locked wheels, low clearance or difficult access.

Should I remove personal items?

Yes. Take important documents, medication, electronics, toll transponders and anything you may need before the vehicle reaches its destination.

What information helps dispatch?

Provide the exact location, destination, vehicle type, problem, damage, wheel condition, key availability and all access restrictions.
