Planning Towing Access at a Commercial Yard in Medley

Commercial properties may have controlled gates, busy loading areas and vehicles that differ from a standard passenger car. Accurate details help determine whether the request fits the available equipment.

Identify the Vehicle Correctly

Provide the vehicle type, approximate size, condition and any load or attached equipment. Do not describe a work truck, van or trailer as a regular car if its dimensions or weight may affect the tow.

Confirm Yard Access

Share the gate number, business name, unit, operating hours and on-site contact. Explain whether a guard, code or manager approval is required.

Clear a Safe Loading Area

Describe forklifts, trailers, parked vehicles, soft ground or materials near the disabled vehicle. The property operator should keep employees away from the loading area and follow site safety rules.

Prepare the Destination

Confirm where the vehicle should go and whether the receiving business is open. Provide billing and authorization details through the company’s normal process.
