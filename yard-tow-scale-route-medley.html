A Yard's Normal Truck Route May Not Reach the Disabled Vehicle

Rapid Towing · October 25, 2026

A commercial property's routine truck route may pass through a scale, checkpoint or loading area that is unrelated to a vehicle pickup. When requesting towing in Medley, ask the site contact which authorized approach applies to the disabled vehicle rather than repeating the usual delivery instructions.

Describe the route's purpose

Explain where the vehicle sits and whether the standard route actually reaches it. Do not bypass a checkpoint or promise access to an area the site has not approved. If you are unsure about restrictions, identify the responsible contact who can answer. Photos or diagrams should be shared only when permitted and should show relevant access without exposing unrelated business information.

Confirm the instructions with dispatch

A route used by another truck is not proof that it suits the equipment required for this vehicle. Pass along the confirmed instructions and any remaining uncertainties. If site staff revise the approach, clearly replace the earlier directions. The goal is one authorized route for the actual pickup, not an assumption that every vehicle entering the yard should follow the same path.
