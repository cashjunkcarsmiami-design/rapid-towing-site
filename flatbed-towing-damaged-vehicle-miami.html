Preparing a Damaged Vehicle for Flatbed Towing in Miami

Flatbed transport may be considered when a vehicle cannot roll normally, has collision damage or requires its wheels to remain off the road. The exact vehicle and location determine the appropriate method.

Report all visible damage

Tell dispatch about broken suspension, bent wheels, deployed airbags, fluid leaks, loose body panels and shattered glass. Explain where the impact occurred and whether the vehicle moved afterward. Photographs from several safe angles can provide more useful context than one close-up image.

Do not stand in traffic or crawl under a damaged vehicle to inspect it. If there is smoke, fire, fuel odor, exposed electrical equipment or injury, move away and contact emergency services.

Confirm whether each wheel can move

State whether the tires hold air, brakes are locked and steering turns. A missing wheel, collapsed suspension or vehicle resting on the ground can affect loading. Also mention whether the keys are available and the transmission can shift into neutral.

Never drag locked drive wheels or improvise release points. Manufacturer instructions and a qualified towing provider should guide transport for the specific model.

Measure garage and property access

A flatbed requires working space and may not fit in every parking structure. Share posted height, parking level, ramp turns and the distance to the exit. For Downtown Miami towing and Miami Beach towing, garage access should be discussed before equipment arrives.

At apartments or gated communities in North Miami and Miami Gardens, confirm management approval, gate width and an authorized service window.

Secure loose items before transport

Remove personal property when it is safe. Tell the operator about detached bumpers, open hoods, damaged doors and contents that may shift. Do not tie the vehicle using an unfamiliar structural or suspension point.

Confirm the destination

Call the repair shop, body shop or storage location and verify that it will accept the vehicle. Ask where it should be left and how keys should be handled after hours. A clear destination prevents an unnecessary second movement.

Flatbed Towing FAQ

Does every damaged car need a flatbed?

No. The correct method depends on the model, drivetrain, damage, wheel condition and manufacturer guidance.

Can a flatbed enter a low parking garage?

Not always. Provide the posted clearance and exact vehicle level so an alternative plan can be considered.

Can an all-wheel-drive vehicle be towed?

Yes, but the correct transport method should follow manufacturer instructions and the actual vehicle condition.
