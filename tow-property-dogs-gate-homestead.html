Dogs Near a Property Gate Before a Homestead Tow

Rapid Towing · October 26, 2026

If dogs have access to the area around a vehicle awaiting pickup in Homestead, discuss that with the property owner before arranging a tow. The aim is to establish a controlled access plan. Do not assume the driver will be able to enter because the animals know you.

Ask the responsible person to arrange access

Identify who can manage the animals and open the agreed entrance. Explain the arrangement to dispatch without promising that the animals will behave a certain way. If you are away from the property, confirm that the responsible person will actually be available for the planned coordination.

Update the plan if conditions change

Tell dispatch if the gate cannot be approached or the animals cannot be kept away from the work area. Do not ask someone to squeeze through a gap or leave an entrance open while waiting. Confirm the practical next step so the vehicle location and the available access match what has been communicated.
