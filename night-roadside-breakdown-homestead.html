What to Do During a Nighttime Breakdown Near Homestead

Dark roads can make a disabled vehicle harder for other drivers and the tow operator to see. Personal safety comes before inspecting or repairing the car.

Make the location visible

Use hazard lights when they work and move away from active lanes only when safe and lawful. Do not stand behind or directly in front of the vehicle. Call 911 for a dangerous traffic position.

Send precise location details

Share a map pin, road name, direction, nearest cross street or mile marker and visible landmarks. Explain whether the car is on pavement, a soft shoulder or near a ditch.

Wait in the safer available place

Follow instructions from emergency personnel and dispatch. Keep your phone charged and avoid walking along an unlit roadway. Report vehicle color, condition and destination.
