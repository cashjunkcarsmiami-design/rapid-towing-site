Coordinating a Building Contact for Towing in North Miami

September 21, 2026

When a vehicle is at a managed building in North Miami, coordinate the pickup with the relevant building contact and towing dispatcher.

Identify who handles vehicle access

Ask the building which person can clarify the entrance and access process for an owner-requested tow. Reception, a garage attendant and property management may have different responsibilities. Explain that you are arranging transport for the specific vehicle; do not assume general visitor access covers a tow truck.

Relay practical restrictions before scheduling

Collect the entrance location, any stated vehicle clearance and the process for meeting the driver. Share known restrictions with Rapid Towing before a truck is selected. Avoid giving an unverified clearance estimate. The building’s access permission and the operator’s equipment assessment are separate matters that both need to be resolved.

Keep the contact available during pickup

Confirm how the driver should reach the building representative and who will meet the vehicle. If a desk shift or access window changes, notify dispatch. Do not rely on the resident being reachable if someone else controls the entrance. Agree on the receiving destination and handoff as part of the same request.

Resumen en español

Consulta quién controla el acceso del edificio y comparte restricciones verificadas con la grúa. Coordina un contacto disponible durante la recogida.
