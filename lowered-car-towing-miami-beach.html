Preparing a Lowered Car for Towing in Miami Beach

Low bumpers, splitters, side skirts and suspension modifications can reduce loading clearance. Accurate photos help identify possible contact points.

Describe every relevant modification

Tell dispatch about lowered suspension, air suspension, wheel size, body kits and existing damage. State whether the suspension can raise and whether the vehicle has a tow mode.

Measure garage restrictions

Provide posted clearance, ramp angle, floor level and space around the car. A truck that fits outside may not fit inside a low structure.

Do not improvise ramps or attachment

Avoid placing loose boards or using body and suspension parts as tow points. Follow manufacturer guidance and let the towing professional determine suitable loading equipment.
