Towing a Car With Steering or Suspension Damage in Hallandale Beach

A vehicle may start after a curb strike or collision but still be unsafe to drive. Bent or displaced components can make the wheels unable to roll normally.

Do not test-drive serious damage

Stop if a wheel sits at an unusual angle, steering binds, the tire rubs or fluid leaks. Driving can worsen damage and reduce control.

Photograph wheel position safely

Take images from the front, side and rear without crawling beneath the vehicle. Report deployed airbags, broken glass, missing parts and whether all wheels touch the ground.

Explain access and destination

Tell dispatch if the car is in traffic, a garage or a tight lot. Give the repair destination and verify it can receive a collision-damaged vehicle after hours.
