Coordinating Towing From a Condo in North Miami

English · Leer en español

Condo properties may control gates, service entrances and garage access. The resident should verify property rules before scheduling.

Before confirming your North Miami pickup

Updated October 29, 2026. Review these details with dispatch so the current plan matches the vehicle and property.

  • Confirm the specific vehicle entrance, not only a resident entrance.
  • Identify who can open the gate if the resident is unavailable.
  • Update dispatch if visitor rules or the access contact changes.

Confirm the service scope, destination, price and current availability directly with Rapid Towing at (305) 457-0817.

Contact Management or Security

Ask whether a tow vehicle needs advance authorization, proof of residency, a vendor entrance or a specific service window.

Describe Garage Conditions

Provide the level, space number, clearance signs, ramp layout and path to an open loading area. Photos can help show tight turns or blocked access.

Confirm Ownership and Keys

Be ready to show that you are authorized to move the vehicle. Report whether the keys are available and whether the car rolls, steers and shifts into neutral.

Coordinate Arrival

Choose an on-site contact who can open gates and meet the driver. Confirm the destination and remove personal belongings from the vehicle.
