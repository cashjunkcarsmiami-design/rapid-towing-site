Identify the Van

Provide the year, make, model, body style and whether it has roof racks, ladders, shelving, trailers or modifications. Mention approximate load and whether loose equipment is inside.

Secure Company Property

Remove valuables and secure tools or materials through the company’s normal process. Do not leave loose objects that may shift during transport.

Describe the Breakdown

Explain whether the van starts, shifts, rolls and steers. Report flat tires, axle or suspension damage, fluid leaks and whether the keys are available.

Confirm Access and Destination

Share garage clearance, gate instructions and the loading space. Verify that the repair facility or business destination can receive the van.
