Requesting Help for a Vehicle with a Trailer in Medley

October 19, 2026

A vehicle connected to a trailer is a different request from a single car pickup. If you need assistance in Medley, explain the complete combination at the first call. Do not assume one tow truck or a standard passenger-car quote covers both units.

Describe both units

Share the towing vehicle model, trailer type and known dimensions. Explain whether the trailer is loaded and whether either unit has damage or movement problems. Photos already taken from a safe position may help dispatch understand the setup, but they do not replace an equipment assessment.

Agree on a plan for each destination

Ask whether the company can handle this specific combination and whether separate transport is needed. Confirm where each unit will go and who can authorize its release. Leave coupling, unloading and recovery decisions to qualified personnel rather than disconnecting equipment simply to speed up arrival.
