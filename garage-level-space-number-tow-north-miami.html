Sharing a Garage Level and Space Number for a North Miami Tow

October 22, 2026

A building address in North Miami may contain several parking levels and entrances. Before arranging towing, confirm the level and space holding the vehicle. A clear description helps the provider assess access instead of discovering important restrictions only after arrival.

Describe the route and restrictions

State the level, space number and authorized vehicle entrance. Mention ramps, height signs or tight turns you can identify. Do not claim that a truck will fit based on a passenger car entering the garage. If the vehicle was relocated by staff, verify its position again and explain whether it can start or roll.

Coordinate with the building contact

Ask who can grant access and whether a service window must be arranged. Keep gate codes in direct communications. If the driver needs to meet someone first, confirm where that should happen. Update dispatch if the car moves to another level or a route closes. A booking inquiry does not override the property's access process.
