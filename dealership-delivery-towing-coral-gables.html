Planning a Tow to a Dealership in Coral Gables

September 21, 2026

Confirm dealership intake details before arranging a vehicle delivery in Coral Gables, including the service entrance and receiving advisor.

Contact the service department directly

A sales desk and a service department do not necessarily share intake procedures. Ask the service team whether it can receive your vehicle on the proposed date. Provide the model and reason for the visit and keep the appointment reference if one is issued. A tow booking does not confirm repair availability or warranty coverage.

Get delivery instructions for the driver

Ask for the service entrance, receiving hours and instructions for the keys. Clarify any after-hours process directly with the dealership before scheduling a late delivery. Share those directions with Rapid Towing along with the pickup details. Do not substitute the main showroom address if the service team specifies another access point.

Confirm arrival and any changed plan

Keep the advisor’s contact available and ask how receipt of the vehicle will be confirmed. If the dealership changes the delivery instructions, inform dispatch before the truck proceeds. Keep transport charges separate from repair estimates and ask each provider about its own services. Retain the tow receipt with the dealership paperwork.
