Planning the Key Handoff for a Tow in Virginia Gardens

September 21, 2026

Make a clear key-handoff plan for a scheduled tow in Virginia Gardens, including who meets the driver and who receives the vehicle.

Tell dispatch which keys are available

Explain whether you have the regular key, a remote fob or a spare, and whether the vehicle responds normally. Do not assume possession of a fob means every needed function works. Describe the situation and let the operator decide what further information is needed. Avoid attempting unfamiliar overrides to simplify the pickup.

Agree on who meets the driver

Confirm who is authorized to hand over the vehicle and how the operator wants the handoff completed. Do not leave keys in an exposed spot based on an assumption that the driver will find them. If you cannot attend, discuss that before the appointment rather than changing the arrangement after dispatch.

Plan delivery and your remaining belongings

Confirm how keys will reach the receiving shop or person. Keep unrelated house keys and personal key rings separate where practical, and account for spare vehicle keys. Ask for confirmation that the handoff is complete. If you change the recipient, notify dispatch so the vehicle and keys follow the same agreed plan.
