Towing From a Gated Property in Hialeah

A tow from an apartment complex, condominium or private lot requires more than a street address. Confirming access before the truck arrives can prevent delays at the entrance.

Give dispatch the complete access plan

Share the property name, gate entrance, building or parking-space number and a working gate code. If security must admit the driver, confirm the office hours and contact person. Mention narrow turns, covered parking, speed bumps or height restrictions.

Describe how the vehicle can be loaded

Tell dispatch whether the keys are present and whether the car rolls, steers and shifts to neutral. Locked wheels, flat tires, collision damage or a vehicle parked tightly between other cars may affect the equipment and approach. Do not attempt to push a disabled vehicle in a crowded lot without a safe plan.

Confirm authorization and destination

Have the property permission and vehicle authorization required for the pickup. Provide the repair shop, home or dealership address and verify that the destination can receive the car, especially after hours.
