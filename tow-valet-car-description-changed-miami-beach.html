When a Valet Record Has the Wrong Vehicle Description

Rapid Towing · October 24, 2026

A valet record may use a shortened model name or an incorrect color. If you are arranging a tow in Miami Beach, resolve any mismatch with the property before relying on that record for pickup. Dispatch and valet staff need to identify the same vehicle.

Compare the actual car with the record

Provide the correct make, model and color and explain the discrepancy to the valet contact. Use the property's approved process to confirm which vehicle can be released. Do not send another guest's ticket or identification to dispatch. If you have not seen the car recently, distinguish the information you know from details the staff still needs to verify.

Confirm the release plan

Ask who will provide keys and where the vehicle will be accessible, without assuming valet will move a disabled car. Tell dispatch about any remaining uncertainty before confirming service. If the vehicle is relocated after the record is corrected, update its position as well. Correcting a label helps identification, but it does not itself establish towing access or authorize release of a vehicle.
