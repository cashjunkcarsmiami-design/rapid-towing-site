Coordinating a Shared Driveway Pickup in West Miami

October 19, 2026

A shared driveway can serve more than one home or parking area. For a scheduled West Miami tow, explain which section leads to your vehicle and whether another household uses the same entrance. A clear address alone may not describe the space needed for pickup.

Coordinate before the arrival window

Let the appropriate household or property contact know about the planned pickup. Confirm who can move any vehicle that blocks access. Give dispatch a callback number for the person who will actually be there, and avoid promising use of a neighboring space without permission.

Update dispatch if access changes

If another vehicle arrives or a delivery blocks the driveway, tell dispatch before the truck approaches. The operator can assess the available space and advise on the next step. Do not direct traffic or move someone else’s vehicle to create an improvised loading area.
