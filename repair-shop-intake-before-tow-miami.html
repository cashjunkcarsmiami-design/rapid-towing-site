Confirm Repair-Shop Intake Before a Tow in Miami

September 21, 2026

Planning a tow to a Miami repair shop? Confirm vehicle acceptance, the delivery entrance and the receiving contact before arranging transport.

Confirm the shop is expecting the vehicle

A shop address is not the same as permission to leave a vehicle there. Call the chosen facility and describe the vehicle and reason for transport. Ask whether it can accept the car on the intended day and whether an appointment is needed. Rapid Towing can discuss transport availability separately from the shop’s repair schedule.

Ask for the correct delivery point

The customer entrance, service lane and after-hours drop area may be different places. Request the exact entrance and the name or role of the person receiving the vehicle. Ask about gates, delivery hours and key handoff instructions. Pass those details to dispatch so the driver does not have to resolve an incomplete destination after loading.

Keep approval and repair work separate

Arranging a tow does not authorize a repair or guarantee the shop’s diagnosis. Keep a record of who confirmed intake and tell the shop if the estimated arrival changes. If it cannot receive the vehicle, agree on a new destination with dispatch before transport continues. Ask about any effect on the quoted towing service.

Resumen en español

Antes de enviar el auto al taller, confirma que lo aceptan, la entrada correcta y quién lo recibirá. El traslado no confirma una cita ni autoriza reparaciones.
