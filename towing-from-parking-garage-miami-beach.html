Towing From a Parking Garage in Miami Beach

A disabled vehicle inside a Miami Beach garage requires more planning than one in an open lot. Low ceilings, tight turns, ramps, access gates and property rules may affect the equipment and pickup process.

Check Height and Entrance Access

Find the posted clearance at the garage entrance and tell dispatch the exact measurement. Identify the correct entrance, parking level, space number and route to the vehicle. Mention sharp turns, steep ramps, narrow lanes, speed bumps or overhead pipes.

Provide the hotel, condominium, municipal garage or business name along with its street address. If a key card, gate code, valet employee or security officer is required, arrange access before the truck arrives.

Describe Whether the Vehicle Can Move

Explain whether the engine starts, the transmission shifts, the steering turns and the wheels roll. A dead battery, flat tire or lockout may sometimes be handled with roadside assistance, while mechanical failure, collision damage or overheating may require towing.

Mention low ground clearance, locked wheels, suspension damage, a missing key or an electric vehicle. Do not push a disabled car down a ramp or attempt unsafe work in an active garage lane.

Confirm Permission and Destination

Ask garage management or property staff about towing authorization, loading areas and temporary access. Rules differ among private properties. Have identification and vehicle information ready when required.

Confirm where the vehicle will be delivered and whether that destination accepts after-hours arrivals. Remove valuables, parking passes and personal documents. Timing depends on traffic, weather, access, demand and equipment, so ask for current scheduling details.

Miami Beach Garage Towing FAQ

Where can I find the garage clearance?

Look for the posted height at the vehicle entrance and share the exact measurement with dispatch.

What if the vehicle will not roll?

Describe locked wheels, steering, tire and suspension condition so the provider can evaluate the situation.

Do I need property permission?

Private garages may have authorization and access rules. Confirm them with management or security when possible.
