When a Car Starts Again Before a Scheduled Tow in Kendall

October 19, 2026

A car that starts again after a failed start can leave you unsure whether to keep a scheduled tow in Kendall. A brief restart does not establish the cause of the problem. Describe the change to dispatch and discuss the vehicle concern with a qualified repair professional.

Report what happened, not a diagnosis

Explain when the car failed to start, whether it later restarted and any warning messages you observed. Do not repeat starting attempts just to demonstrate the fault. If you have a service appointment, ask the shop whether it still recommends transport based on the information you can provide.

Update the booking explicitly

If you decide to change or cancel the tow, contact dispatch directly and ask for confirmation and any applicable terms. Do not assume a missed call cancels the request. If you keep the pickup, explain the current condition so the driver is not working from an outdated description.
