Matching a Tow to a Dealership Appointment in Coral Gables

October 19, 2026

A repair appointment and a vehicle delivery are related but separate arrangements. Before towing to a dealership in Coral Gables, confirm that the service department expects a non-driving vehicle. A booked appointment does not always explain the unloading location or the key handoff procedure.

Contact the service advisor

Provide the vehicle details and appointment reference to the receiving department. Ask which entrance accepts towed vehicles and who will take the keys. Confirm whether the dealership can receive the car before the appointment time, rather than assuming early delivery is allowed.

Share only the transport details

Give dispatch the approved delivery address and receiving contact. Keep repair approvals and warranty questions with the dealership. If the service advisor changes the location or receiving time, update dispatch before the vehicle departs so the driver has one agreed destination.
