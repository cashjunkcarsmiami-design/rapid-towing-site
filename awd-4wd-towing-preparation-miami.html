MIAMI TOWING GUIDE

AWD and 4WD Towing in Miami: What to Tell Dispatch

October 14, 2026

When an all-wheel-drive or four-wheel-drive vehicle needs transport, the exact model matters. A badge on the tailgate is a starting point, not a complete towing instruction. Before requesting a tow in Miami or Doral, give dispatch the year, make, model and drivetrain information you know so the loading and transport request can be reviewed properly.

This guide helps you prepare information for a professional tow. It does not provide a procedure for shifting a transfer case, releasing a transmission or towing a vehicle behind another car. Use the owner’s manual for the exact vehicle and discuss the requirements with the towing operator.

Identify the vehicle before choosing equipment

Say whether the car is AWD or 4WD when known. If you are unsure, say so instead of assuming that every trim of the same model uses the same drivetrain. Include any hybrid or electric configuration, available keys and whether the vehicle starts. Tell dispatch if the car has been modified or has unusual ground clearance.

The information helps frame the equipment discussion. Asking for flatbed towing is useful, but availability and the complete loading plan still need confirmation for the particular car and pickup location.

Locate the correct manufacturer instructions

Look for the towing or transporting section in the owner’s manual for your model year. Emergency recovery and recreational towing can describe different circumstances; do not treat instructions for pulling a vehicle behind a motorhome as a general loading plan after a breakdown.

For example, this Ford owner’s manual recommends all wheels off the ground for the four-wheel-drive vehicles it covers. That is a model-specific reference, not a rule for every AWD or 4WD car. Provide your own vehicle’s instructions to the operator when available.

Describe movement separately from drivetrain

An AWD badge does not tell dispatch whether the wheels roll, the steering works or a wheel has collision damage. Describe those conditions independently. If they have not been checked, report them as unknown. Mention flat tires, missing wheels, locked access or visible suspension damage.

Do not force the vehicle into a loading position or try an unfamiliar release procedure just to request a quote. Share the observed limitation and let the operator assess the transport needs.

Explain the pickup space and destination

Provide the exact pickup address and the vehicle’s position within the property. A Doral commercial lot, a condo garage and a driveway can involve different access restrictions. Give any available height information and describe gates, parked cars or a narrow turn without promising that a particular truck will fit.

Confirm where the vehicle is going and whether that location has agreed to receive it. Keep the manufacturer requirements and the actual access details together rather than choosing equipment based only on a photo of the car.

Confirm the complete request

Ask dispatch to confirm equipment availability, the pickup arrangement, destination and quoted scope before authorizing service. Update the request if the vehicle condition, location or receiver changes. A conversation about a possible tow is not the same as a confirmed arrival time.

Resumen en español

Si tu carro es AWD o 4WD, indica año, marca, modelo y lo que sabes de la tracción. Busca las instrucciones de transporte del fabricante para ese vehículo. Explica por separado si rueda, si gira la dirección y cómo es el acceso. No fuerces cambios ni procedimientos desconocidos: confirma con despacho el equipo y las condiciones de la grúa.
