A Dealership Sales Entrance May Not Be the Vehicle Delivery Point

Rapid Towing · October 26, 2026

A dealership's main entrance often serves people arriving to speak with sales staff. For a tow to a Coral Gables dealership, confirm where the service department wants the vehicle delivered. A familiar street address alone may not identify the appropriate receiving area.

Ask the receiving department

Provide the vehicle details and ask for the current delivery location and contact. If there is a separate service entrance, pass that information to dispatch. Do not rely solely on a photo of the showroom or on instructions from a previous visit made for a different purpose.

Keep the receiving confirmation together

Record the contact name, location and any confirmed arrival instructions with the tow request. If the shop changes its instructions, update dispatch before the vehicle is moved. Confirming the correct receiving point helps distinguish where a customer can walk in from where the business has agreed to accept the towed vehicle.
