Folded Mirrors in Photos of a Vehicle Awaiting a Hialeah Tow

Rapid Towing · October 26, 2026

A folded mirror can look missing or damaged in a photograph taken from an angle. When sending vehicle photos for a Hialeah tow, explain whether the mirror is folded normally, hanging loose or in an unknown condition. These descriptions are more useful than leaving dispatch to interpret a small image.

Describe what you can observe

If you know the mirror was folded for parking, say so. If it moved after damage or you do not know why it is positioned that way, state that instead. Do not force a stiff housing back into place to improve the photo or claim that its adjustment mechanism works without checking.

Include surrounding context

A wider view can show which side of the car is pictured and whether nearby objects limit access. Mention any loose piece that you can see from an accessible position. Dispatch can then consider the current condition alongside the location and destination, without mistaking a photo angle for a confirmed equipment problem.
