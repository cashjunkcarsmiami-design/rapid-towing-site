When an Overheated Car Needs Towing in Kendall

Steam, a high temperature gauge or an overheating warning means the vehicle needs immediate attention. Continuing to drive may worsen engine damage and can leave you stopped in a more dangerous place.

Stop and let the vehicle cool

Move out of traffic only if the car can be moved safely, turn off the engine and keep people away from traffic. Never remove a hot radiator or coolant cap: pressurized coolant can cause severe burns. Call emergency services if there is fire, injury or an immediate roadway hazard.

Know when transport is the safer choice

A persistent warning, visible leak, steam, loss of power or repeated overheating should be evaluated by a qualified repair professional. Do not rely on repeatedly adding coolant or restarting the engine when the cause is unknown.

Details to give the towing dispatcher

Share your exact Kendall location, vehicle year and model, warning symptoms, whether fluid is leaking and whether the car can roll and steer. Give the repair destination and mention low clearance or parking-garage access.
