Two Driveways at One Address: Planning a Vehicle Pickup

Rapid Towing · October 25, 2026

A home can have more than one driveway or access point. For a planned tow in Virginia Gardens, specify which area contains the vehicle and which entrance is authorized. A street address alone may lead the operator to the wrong side of the property.

Use practical visible references

Describe the vehicle's position relative to a feature you can identify accurately. Avoid vague instructions such as the usual driveway if dispatch has never visited. Do not share household access codes in public or direct anyone through a neighbor's property. If you do not know which entrance is suitable, tell dispatch and ask what information is needed.

Distinguish the meeting place from the car

Explain where the key holder will wait and whether that is different from the vehicle location. Do not move a disabled car simply to match a suggested navigation pin. If the property contact changes the available entrance, update the plan before pickup is treated as ready. Clear descriptions help dispatch assess access using the actual circumstances rather than an assumed route.
