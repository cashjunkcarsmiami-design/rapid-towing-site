Arranging a Scheduled Tow for a Family Member in Hialeah

September 21, 2026

Coordinate a Hialeah towing request when the caller and the person with the vehicle are different people. Confirm contacts, access and destination.

Explain who is making the request

Tell Rapid Towing whether you own the vehicle, are helping a relative or are coordinating with the person at the pickup. Ask what permission or identification the operator needs for that arrangement. Do not assume that paying for transport is enough to establish authorization to release a vehicle.

Agree on a shared set of instructions

Confirm the pickup address, vehicle description and destination with the family member before calling. Establish who has the keys and who can answer questions at arrival. Share the relevant phone numbers directly with dispatch, with the people’s agreement. Avoid separate conversations that give the driver conflicting destination or timing instructions.

Plan the handoff without assuming a ride

Ask who must be present for collection and how the keys will be transferred. Discuss passenger arrangements separately; do not assume the tow truck can carry everyone. Request an estimated arrival window and an update if it changes. Keep both contacts informed if the vehicle moves or the receiving location becomes unavailable.

Resumen en español

Si pides una grúa para un familiar, confirma quién autoriza, quién tiene las llaves y quién estará en la recogida. Consulta aparte el transporte de pasajeros.
