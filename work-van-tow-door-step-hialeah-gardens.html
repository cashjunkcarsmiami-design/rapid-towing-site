An Added Side Step on a Work Van Awaiting Towing

Rapid Towing · October 26, 2026

A work van in Hialeah Gardens may have an added step below a side door. Include that accessory when describing the vehicle for towing, particularly if it extends beyond the body or appears damaged. A standard model description may not explain equipment installed after the van was built.

Show the accessory in context

If you can take photos safely, include a side view showing the step's location relative to the door and wheels. Describe whether it is fixed, retractable or unknown. Do not operate a mechanism you are unfamiliar with simply to demonstrate it, and do not assume a bent step can support weight.

Let dispatch assess the current configuration

Mention other relevant modifications and explain any limits to what you can observe. Ask dispatch to consider equipment suitability with those details included. If someone removes or changes the step before pickup, provide an update so the planned service reflects the van as it will actually be presented.
