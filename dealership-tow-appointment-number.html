A Dealership Appointment Number for a Coral Gables Tow

Rapid Towing · October 23, 2026

A service appointment number can help a dealership identify your visit, but it is not necessarily a delivery authorization. Before sending a vehicle to a dealership in Coral Gables, confirm that the receiving team expects it to arrive by tow.

Explain how the vehicle will arrive

Ask which entrance and department should receive it and whether your appointment information is sufficient for intake. Tell them if the vehicle cannot move under its own power. Do not assume a booking made for a drive-in visit accounts for unloading space or a disabled vehicle.

Share confirmed instructions with dispatch

Provide the receiving contact and the actual delivery details rather than only the appointment number. If the dealership changes the appointment, confirm whether the tow should also change. Keep the vehicle's condition and access limitations current. A destination selected in a form does not guarantee acceptance; resolve unanswered intake questions before authorizing transport to that location.
