Updating a Building Contact for a Downtown Miami Tow

October 22, 2026

A towing pickup in Downtown Miami can depend on a building employee opening a gate or guiding access. If that person changes shifts, the new contact needs accurate instructions. A message sent to someone who has left may not reach the person controlling entry.

Identify who can authorize access at the planned time

Ask the building which desk or contact handles vehicle-service arrivals. Confirm whether any service window or check-in process applies. Explain the car's location and condition without assuming staff can move it. Keep private access details out of public posts and share only the information dispatch needs.

Replace outdated instructions

Tell dispatch when the contact changes and confirm a phone number that will be answered. If the access window also changes, ask whether the pickup can still be arranged and whether terms need updating. Do not assume a driver's arrival estimate grants permission to use a loading area. Reconfirm the vehicle's position if staff relocate it before collection.
