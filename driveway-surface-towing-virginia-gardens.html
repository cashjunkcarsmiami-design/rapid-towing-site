Describing a Driveway Surface Before a Virginia Gardens Tow

October 19, 2026

For a home pickup in Virginia Gardens, the driveway surface can matter as much as the street address. Tell dispatch about recently installed paving, loose gravel or a property restriction on heavy vehicles. The driver needs to assess access rather than discover an unexpected limit at the entrance.

Describe the approach honestly

Mention visible slopes, tight turns and low branches from your normal safe viewpoint. If you know the property has a posted restriction, relay its exact wording. Do not guess load capacity or promise that a decorative surface will support a truck.

Let the operator assess placement

Ask what access information is needed before the visit. Keep household vehicles clear only when they can be moved safely, and coordinate permission with the property owner. Do not move the disabled car to the street yourself solely to avoid a driveway discussion.
