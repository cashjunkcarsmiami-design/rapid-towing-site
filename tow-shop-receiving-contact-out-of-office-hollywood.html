Your Repair Adviser Is Away When a Vehicle Needs Delivery

Rapid Towing · October 25, 2026

An appointment may have been arranged with a repair adviser who is unavailable when the vehicle is ready for towing. Before confirming delivery in Hollywood, ask the shop who can receive it in that person's absence. A previous conversation does not identify the current receiving contact by itself.

Confirm the substitute arrangement

Use the shop's normal contact channel and explain the vehicle and appointment reference. Ask who can accept the car and keys, and whether the original intake instructions still apply. Do not assume another employee has seen your messages or can authorize every arrangement discussed with the adviser. Keep any uncertainty visible to dispatch.

Update the tow request with confirmed details

Provide the current receiving contact and explain which earlier instruction has changed. Ask whether any change affects coordination or the estimate. If no one can confirm acceptance, do not treat the destination as ready solely because the business is open. A clear receiving plan should identify a person or agreed process that actually applies when the vehicle arrives.
