Preparing a Tow Request From a Low-Clearance Garage in Coral Gables

Garage height and tight ramps can affect equipment access. Report the posted clearance and vehicle position before dispatch.

Photograph the Clearance Sign

Send the posted entrance height and do not estimate it from memory. Mention pipes, signs or lower sections inside the garage.

Map the Route Out

Describe the parking level, ramp direction, sharp turns, gate controls and distance to an open area. Explain whether another car blocks the vehicle.

Report Vehicle Movement

Tell dispatch whether the vehicle starts, rolls, steers, has keys and can shift into neutral. Mention flat tires, low ground clearance or collision damage.

Confirm Property Approval

Ask management about vendor access and service hours. Arrange for someone authorized to open gates and meet the driver.
