Move Away From Traffic When Possible

If the vehicle still moves safely, pull into a parking lot, side street or shoulder and turn on the hazard lights. Do not attempt a repair beside fast traffic or stand between vehicles. If there is a collision, injury, fire or immediate danger, contact emergency services first.

Drivers on the Palmetto Expressway, Okeechobee Road or other busy routes should identify the direction of travel and nearest exit. In a commercial lot, share the business name and the section where the vehicle is parked.

Identify the Roadside Problem

Check only what can be observed safely. A dead battery may need a jump start, a usable spare may allow a tire change, an empty tank may call for fuel delivery, and keys locked inside may require lockout help. When the vehicle has collision damage, overheating, steering problems or a mechanical failure, towing may be the safer option.

Tell dispatch whether the vehicle starts, rolls, steers and shifts to neutral. Mention a flat tire, damaged wheel, fluid leak, low clearance, missing key or blocked access. This information helps the provider evaluate equipment needs.

Prepare for Towing in Hialeah

Remove valuables, medication, documents and electronic devices. Confirm the destination address and whether the repair shop can receive the car. If the vehicle is inside a garage, behind a gate or on private property, provide access information and any height restriction.

Service timing depends on location, traffic, weather, current demand and equipment. Ask for the available details when scheduling and keep your phone nearby so the driver can locate you.

Hialeah Roadside FAQ

What location details should I provide?

Share the address or map pin, nearest intersection or exit, direction of travel and a visible landmark.

Should I try to change a tire beside traffic?

Do not attempt a tire change where you cannot work safely away from moving traffic.

What should I remove before towing?

Take valuables, documents, medication, electronics and any items you may need before the vehicle arrives at its destination.
