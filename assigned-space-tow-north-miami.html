Identifying an Assigned Parking Space for a North Miami Tow

September 28, 2026

In a property with several parking areas, the street address may only get a driver to the building. A precise space description helps the property contact and dispatch discuss the same pickup location.

Identify the parking area

Give the building address along with the level, zone or space number you know. State whether the vehicle is indoors or outside. Avoid guessing a clearance measurement or assuming that every entrance leads to the same area.

Confirm access with the property contact

Ask the relevant manager or attendant how the scheduled pickup should be handled. Share the agreed entrance instructions with dispatch. An assigned space does not by itself answer whether a particular truck can enter or where loading can be assessed.

Report a relocated vehicle

If the vehicle is moved to another space before pickup, update the request with the new location. Confirm that the person holding the keys knows about the change. Keeping the original address while omitting the new space can leave dispatch looking for a car that is no longer where expected.

Resumen en español

Indica edificio, nivel y número de espacio si los conoces. Confirma la entrada con el encargado y avisa si el vehículo cambia de lugar antes de la recogida.
