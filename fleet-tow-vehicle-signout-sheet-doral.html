A Fleet Sign-Out Sheet Still Lists the Disabled Vehicle as Available

Rapid Towing · October 26, 2026

Before a business tow in Doral, check whether the vehicle remains available on the company's sign-out sheet. Another employee may plan to use it while you are arranging transport. The internal availability record and the towing conversation should describe the same vehicle status.

Notify the person managing assignments

Identify the unit clearly and explain that a towing request is being coordinated. Ask who can update its availability and who holds the keys. Do not assume that sending dispatch a message automatically updates the company's records or alerts a driver who has already reserved that unit.

Keep operational changes connected

If someone moves the vehicle, transfers equipment or changes the receiving shop, update dispatch before the service proceeds. Confirm who is authorized to approve the tow and who will answer practical questions. A brief internal handoff helps prevent a truck from being directed to a location the vehicle has already left.
