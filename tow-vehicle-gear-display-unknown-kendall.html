An Unclear Gear Display Before a Kendall Tow Request

Rapid Towing · October 25, 2026

A dark or unreadable gear display leaves an important uncertainty when describing a disabled vehicle. If you are requesting towing in Kendall, tell dispatch that you cannot confirm the displayed position. Do not guess from memory or try a sequence of controls simply to provide an answer.

Explain what you can observe

Identify the make and model and say whether the display is blank, partially visible or showing something you do not understand. Mention any relevant condition already known. Do not claim the vehicle is ready to roll because the selector looks familiar. A photo can help explain the display if it can be taken safely without operating the car.

Ask what information is still needed

Dispatch must assess suitability and equipment based on the vehicle and its actual limitations. Do not follow generic shifting instructions from a different model to prepare a pickup. If another qualified person confirms the state later, update dispatch and identify what changed. Keep the distinction between a known position and an unverified assumption clear before authorizing the service.
