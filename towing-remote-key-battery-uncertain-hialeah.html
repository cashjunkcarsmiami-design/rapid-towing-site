When a Remote Key Does Not Respond Before a Hialeah Tow

October 22, 2026

A remote key that does not respond can complicate access to a vehicle in Hialeah. It does not, by itself, identify the cause or show whether the car can be moved. Explain what keys or remotes you have and what you can actually access before requesting towing equipment.

Report observations instead of a diagnosis

Tell dispatch whether the doors are locked, whether another key is available and when the vehicle was last used. Do not assume the problem is only the remote's battery unless that has been established. Avoid forcing locks or trying improvised electrical connections. If you cannot check the steering or transmission position, say that those details are unknown.

Arrange access before confirming the pickup

Ask what information the provider needs to evaluate the tow in its present condition. Explain if personal belongings remain inaccessible and whether another person holds a spare key. Do not promise an additional key pickup without agreement. Update dispatch if access becomes available, because the plan should reflect the vehicle as it is at collection.
