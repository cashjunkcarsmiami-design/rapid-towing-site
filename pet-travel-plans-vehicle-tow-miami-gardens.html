Planning Separate Transportation for Pets During a Miami Gardens Tow

October 22, 2026

A vehicle problem in Miami Gardens can disrupt plans for everyone traveling with you, including pets. Tell dispatch about your situation, but make a separate transportation plan for the animals. Do not assume a towing vehicle can carry pets, carriers or additional passengers.

Ask about limitations before relying on a ride

Explain how many people need transportation and that a pet is with you. Ask what, if any, passenger arrangements are available for the specific service; acceptance must be confirmed. Coordinate a suitable alternative with someone who can help. Do not leave an animal in the vehicle being transported or treat the tow as an animal transport service.

Keep the handoff organized

When safe to do so, gather leashes, carriers and essential personal items before the authorized pickup. Let dispatch know if another person will meet you so the contact plan remains clear. Keep the towing destination separate from where you and the pet need to go. Confirm the vehicle arrangements without assuming additional stops or waiting time are included.
