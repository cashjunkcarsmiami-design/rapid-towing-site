Before confirming your Downtown Miami pickup

Updated October 29, 2026. Review these details with dispatch so the current plan matches the vehicle and property.

  • Separate the building street address from the approved vehicle entrance.
  • Confirm who remains available if reception closes before pickup.
  • Update the parking level or space if building staff relocates the car.

Confirm the service scope, destination, price and current availability directly with Rapid Towing at (305) 457-0817.

Identify the Garage and Level

Share the building name, entrance street, parking level, space number and nearest elevator or landmark. Confirm which entrance commercial vehicles may use.

Report the Posted Height

Photograph the clearance sign and mention lower pipes or structures inside. Do not assume standard towing equipment can enter every garage.

Describe the Vehicle

Report whether it starts, rolls, steers and has keys. Mention flat tires, locked wheels, low clearance, collision damage or an electric drivetrain.

Coordinate Building Access

Ask security or management about gate authorization and service hours. Confirm an open loading area if the vehicle can be moved safely.
