Rescheduling a Planned Tow in Hollywood

September 28, 2026

A planned tow may need to change because a shop, vehicle owner or property contact becomes unavailable. A clear rescheduling request helps distinguish a new plan from a second, duplicate pickup request.

Refer to the existing arrangement

Give dispatch the vehicle and original pickup details, then explain what has changed. State whether you are requesting a different time or asking to cancel. Do not assume silence, a missed call or an unconfirmed message updates the original request.

Check both ends of the trip

Before suggesting a replacement window, ask the pickup contact and receiving location about availability. A convenient time for you may not work for the shop accepting the car. Keep any proposed window tentative until dispatch confirms it can accommodate the request.

Confirm the final status

Ask whether the original arrangement has been replaced and whether any terms or charges apply to the change. Share the confirmed update with everyone involved. Retain the callback details so further changes reach the correct request rather than starting several separate conversations about the same vehicle.

Resumen en español

Para cambiar una grúa programada, identifica la solicitud original y consulta recogida y destino. Espera confirmación del nuevo horario y pregunta si aplica alguna condición por el cambio.
