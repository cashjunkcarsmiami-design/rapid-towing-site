A Map Pin on a Rear Service Road in West Miami

Rapid Towing · October 24, 2026

A navigation pin can appear near a vehicle while directing the approach to a road that does not provide access. When requesting towing in West Miami, explain whether the car is reached from the front street, rear service road or another confirmed entrance. Proximity on a map is not enough.

Describe the usable approach

Provide the full address and a visible reference for the entrance you are authorized to use. If you do not know which approach accommodates the service, say so and ask dispatch what information is needed. Do not direct an operator through a private route simply because the map suggests it. Photos should be taken only from a safe, permitted location.

Confirm where the vehicle actually waits

Distinguish the building address from the car's position and the place where you will meet the operator. If someone moved the vehicle, update those details before relying on the earlier pin. Do not reposition a disabled car to match the navigation suggestion. A clear description lets dispatch assess the route with the actual location and access limitations in mind.
