Using a Digital Key During a Hialeah Towing Pickup

October 19, 2026

If your vehicle uses a phone app or key card, explain that when arranging a tow in Hialeah. Access to the passenger compartment and the ability to prepare a car for transport are separate questions. A successful remote unlock does not confirm that every towing requirement is met.

Identify the available key

Tell dispatch whether you have a physical key, card, paired phone or only remote account access. Describe any access problem already observed. Use the vehicle manufacturer instructions for your model; do not experiment with transport settings or assume an app will work without connectivity.

Plan the receiving handoff

Ask the receiving shop which key it needs and who will receive it. Keep account passwords private and arrange an appropriate authorized handoff instead of sending login credentials. If you cannot be present, agree on a contact and access plan before the truck is sent.
