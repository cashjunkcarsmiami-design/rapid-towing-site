A Repair Order Number Belongs to Another Car

Rapid Towing · October 25, 2026

A household or business may have several vehicles at the same service provider. Before arranging a tow to Coral Gables, verify that any repair order or appointment reference belongs to the vehicle being transported. A valid reference can still describe the wrong car.

Check with the receiving contact

Provide the correct vehicle description and ask the shop to confirm the applicable reference and intake arrangement. Do not send another customer's paperwork to dispatch or use an old order simply because the business name matches. If the shop has not created a reference, say that rather than inventing one to complete the towing request.

Correct the destination notes explicitly

If you already supplied a wrong number, tell dispatch which detail must be replaced. Confirm that the address, receiving contact and key arrangement still apply. A corrected reference does not automatically establish that the shop can receive the vehicle at any time. Keep those conditions separate and ask about any remaining questions before authorizing transport based on the final receiving instructions.
