Details That Help Before Dispatch

When Towing Is Safer Than Driving

Request towing when the vehicle has crash damage, steering or brake problems, severe overheating, a damaged wheel, fluid loss, or another condition that makes driving unsafe. For a simple dead battery or lockout in a safe location, roadside assistance may be enough.

Stay Safe While Waiting

Turn on hazard lights when appropriate and remain away from moving traffic. Do not stand between vehicles or attempt repairs beside an active lane. If anyone is injured or the location presents an immediate danger, contact emergency services first.
