A Digital Valet Ticket Link Has Expired Before a Miami Beach Tow

Rapid Towing · October 26, 2026

If a digital valet ticket link no longer opens, contact the valet operator before coordinating a tow in Miami Beach. An expired link may make the parking record harder to retrieve, but it does not establish where the car or its keys are currently held.

Use the operator's current process

Explain the issue and ask what information is needed to locate your vehicle record. Provide details through the operator's appropriate channel rather than forwarding unrelated personal documents to everyone involved. Ask the valet contact to confirm the car's location and the process for making it available.

Tell dispatch what is confirmed

Share the practical pickup instructions once you receive them. If the parking record is still being resolved, say that access remains pending. Do not treat an old screenshot as proof that release arrangements are complete. A current answer from the responsible operator is more useful than repeated attempts to open the same expired link.
