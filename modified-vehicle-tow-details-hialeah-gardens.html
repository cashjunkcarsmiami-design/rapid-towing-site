Describing a Modified Vehicle Before Towing in Hialeah Gardens

October 19, 2026

A vehicle with modifications may need a different assessment from the standard model. Before requesting a Hialeah Gardens pickup, tell dispatch about changes to ride height, wheel size or bodywork. A model name alone does not communicate the vehicle’s current configuration.

Describe changes without guessing clearance

Mention lowered suspension, oversized tires, extended bumpers or other visible additions. Provide known dimensions if you already have them, but do not crawl under a disabled vehicle to measure. Tell dispatch about existing damage that could affect loading as a separate issue from the modifications.

Confirm capability before dispatch

Ask whether the operator can evaluate your specific setup and what additional information is needed. Do not assume standard loading equipment will fit or attempt to remove bodywork yourself. Share the same details with the receiving shop so the delivery plan reflects the vehicle that will actually arrive.
