When Valet Keys Are Held Elsewhere Before a Miami Beach Tow

October 22, 2026

A car may be parked in one part of a Miami Beach property while its keys are kept at another desk or facility. Clarify that arrangement before booking a tow. The person who can locate the vehicle may not be the person who can release its keys.

Ask the valet or property contact directly

Confirm the current parking position, key location and release process for your vehicle. Explain whether it starts or has a mobility problem. Do not promise that staff can drive it to the entrance if that has not been confirmed. Avoid sending unrelated guest or room information when a vehicle reference is sufficient.

Agree on the handoff location

Ask where the authorized person will meet the driver and whether keys will be brought to that point. Do not assume the tow includes another trip to retrieve them. If staff change shifts, recheck who is responsible. Keep permission to access the garage distinct from permission to release the vehicle, and resolve both before treating the pickup as ready.
