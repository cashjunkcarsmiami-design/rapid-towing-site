An Old Valet Photo Before a Miami Beach Vehicle Tow

Rapid Towing · October 23, 2026

A photo from valet check-in may show the vehicle before it was moved to another area. When arranging a tow in Miami Beach, distinguish that record from a current view of where the vehicle is parked.

Ask for the present position

Confirm the level, space and vehicle access route with the responsible staff member. Explain if the photo is from arrival rather than today. Do not assume the visible driveway in the image is where loading will occur. If current photos cannot be provided, report that limitation to dispatch.

Keep condition and location updates separate

A vehicle can remain in the same condition while its access changes. Tell dispatch about either kind of update, including a move behind other vehicles or into a different garage area. Confirm key release and the meeting contact before the pickup. The old photo can support identification, but equipment and access need assessment against the actual position at the time of collection.
