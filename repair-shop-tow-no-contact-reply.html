When the Receiving Shop Has Not Replied Before a Hollywood Tow

Rapid Towing · October 23, 2026

A repair shop's unanswered message leaves an important delivery question unresolved. Before a planned tow in Hollywood, distinguish your request for intake from confirmation that the shop will receive the vehicle.

Ask for the details you still need

Confirm acceptance, arrival instructions and the person or process responsible for keys. Do not assume business hours alone guarantee that a disabled vehicle can be unloaded. If you have an appointment but no towing instructions, explain that to both the shop and dispatch.

Avoid an unconfirmed delivery plan

Tell dispatch which details remain pending before authorizing transport. Ask what options can be considered if the shop cannot receive the vehicle, without treating an alternative destination as approved automatically. If a reply arrives later, share the confirmed instructions and review any change in the request. A message marked delivered is not the same as a shop accepting the vehicle.

Resumen en español

Un mensaje recibido por el taller no confirma que acepten el vehículo. Aclara recepción, descarga y llaves antes de autorizar el traslado.
