Building Access Depends on a Phone App: What to Tell Dispatch

Rapid Towing · October 25, 2026

Some residents use a phone application to manage building access. If you are arranging a tow in North Miami, explain any dependency on your availability before confirming the pickup. Do not assume an operator can use your account or that access can be transferred simply by forwarding a message.

Ask the property about authorized options

Confirm with the appropriate building contact how a vehicle pickup should be handled. Do not share your password, verification codes or unrestricted account access. Identify who will be present and able to follow the property's process. If the app is unavailable, say so and ask the property what approved alternative exists rather than improvising one.

Pass along only the relevant plan

Tell dispatch the confirmed approach, the contact who can assist and any unresolved restrictions. Access permission and equipment suitability are separate questions; an app opening a gate does not confirm clearance for the service. If your phone or availability changes, update the arrangement. The operator should receive a practical, authorized contact plan instead of an instruction that depends on credentials they should not have.
