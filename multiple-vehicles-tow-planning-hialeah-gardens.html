Requesting Transport for Multiple Vehicles in Hialeah Gardens

September 21, 2026

Organize a multi-vehicle towing inquiry in Hialeah Gardens with a separate description, pickup and destination for each vehicle.

List each vehicle separately

Provide the make, model and condition of every vehicle rather than describing the job only as a number of cars. Note which vehicles have keys and whether their pickup locations differ. Mention any modifications or access limitations. A multi-vehicle request does not mean one truck can carry every vehicle in a single trip.

Confirm sequence and equipment availability

Tell dispatch which move is most time-sensitive and why. Ask what scheduling and equipment options are available for the specific request. Do not promise a shared arrival or completion time to the receiving locations before that plan is confirmed. If a vehicle becomes unavailable, update the list so the remaining arrangements can be reviewed.

Keep destinations and charges unambiguous

Assign a clear destination and receiving contact to each vehicle. Ask whether pricing is quoted separately or as an agreed combined scope, and what changes would require a new estimate. Confirm key handoff for every pickup. Keep a simple record of each completed move so there is no confusion about which vehicle has arrived.

Resumen en español

Para varios vehículos, detalla cada unidad, destino y contacto. Confirma equipos, orden y cotización; no supongas que una sola grúa puede llevarlos todos.
