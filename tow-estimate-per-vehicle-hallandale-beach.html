Does the Towing Estimate Cover One Vehicle or Two?

Rapid Towing · October 24, 2026

If a conversation mentions multiple vehicles, make sure a towing estimate identifies which one it covers. In Hallandale Beach, two cars at the same location do not automatically form a single service request. Their condition, destination and transport requirements may differ.

Describe each request separately

Give each vehicle a clear reference using make, model and color. State its destination and any known access or movement limitations. Ask whether the proposed amount applies to one vehicle, both, or only a stated part of the work. Do not assume that sharing a pickup address means the vehicles can travel together or that the same equipment is appropriate.

Authorize only the confirmed scope

Request a written summary that connects each quoted amount to the correct vehicle and service. If you decide to proceed with only one, say which request should remain active and ask dispatch to acknowledge the change. Confirm availability and coordination separately. A clear scope avoids leaving a second vehicle expected for pickup when the actual agreement only covers the first.
