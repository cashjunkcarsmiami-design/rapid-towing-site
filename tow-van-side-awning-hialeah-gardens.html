A Side Awning on a Work Van Before Towing

Rapid Towing · October 25, 2026

A van may carry an exterior awning or similar attached equipment that is not obvious in a frontal photograph. When requesting towing in Hialeah Gardens, describe that equipment and its current condition. Do not assume it is irrelevant because it is normally folded against the vehicle.

Explain the installed configuration

State whether the accessory is folded, extended, damaged or unverified. Do not climb onto the vehicle or operate unfamiliar mechanisms to prepare a photo. If a safe side view is available, include it with the make and model. Avoid guessing dimensions or claiming the installation is secure when you have not confirmed that condition.

Ask about preparation and suitability

Dispatch needs the complete configuration to assess equipment and access. Do not assume folding, removing or repairing accessories is included in towing. Have the responsible person clarify what can be prepared appropriately before service is finalized. If the configuration changes after the estimate, provide an update and ask whether the plan needs review. The request should describe the vehicle as it will actually be presented for pickup.
