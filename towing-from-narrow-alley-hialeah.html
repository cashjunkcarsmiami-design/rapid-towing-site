Planning a Tow From a Narrow Alley in Hialeah

Alleys behind homes and businesses can limit turning space and equipment access. Photos and measurements help dispatch understand the approach.

Describe both alley entrances

Give the nearest streets, gate location, width, overhead wires, dumpsters and parked vehicles. Identify whether the truck can enter from one side and exit from the other.

Explain the vehicle position

State which direction the car faces, whether it rolls and steers, and if keys are present. Mention flat tires, locked wheels or collision damage.

Clear authorized access

Move bins and vehicles you control, keep people away and obtain permission before using another property. Do not attach straps or push a disabled car through a confined space without professional direction.
