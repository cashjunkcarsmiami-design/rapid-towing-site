A New Security Contact for a Downtown Miami Tow

Rapid Towing · October 24, 2026

A security team change can make an earlier access arrangement difficult to recognize. If you are coordinating a tow from an office building in Downtown Miami, confirm that the contact currently on duty knows the plan. A conversation with yesterday's staff may not reach the person handling access today.

Share the minimum useful details

Ask the building contact what reference is needed for the vehicle and pickup. Explain the current location, responsible person and known restrictions without publishing private building credentials. Do not assume a security employee can authorize removal of any vehicle; keep the vehicle owner's approval and property access arrangement clearly separate.

Update dispatch with confirmed instructions

Provide a working contact and identify any changed entry directions. If the new staff cannot confirm the arrangement, say it is still pending rather than presenting access as ready. Avoid sending several staff numbers with no explanation of who is responsible. One current contact plan helps dispatch coordinate the handoff and lets the operator ask the right person about the property when service is arranged.
