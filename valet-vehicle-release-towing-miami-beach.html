Arranging a Tow for a Valet-Parked Car in Miami Beach

September 21, 2026

Coordinate with the valet operator before requesting a tow for a parked vehicle in Miami Beach. Confirm its location and release process.

Find out where the vehicle actually is

A valet stand may not be where the car is stored. Ask the valet operator for the vehicle’s current location and tell dispatch whether it is in a garage or an off-site lot. Explain the issue to the valet team before anyone tries to relocate the car. Let the towing operator assess the transport requirements.

Clarify who can release the keys and car

Ask the valet operator what it requires for an owner-requested release. Have the relevant claim reference available and confirm who will meet the driver. Do not assume a message to a hotel receptionist reaches the valet team. Keep responsibility for authorization and key handoff clear between all parties.

Coordinate the pickup point with dispatch

Share access and clearance information supplied by the property, and identify the approved loading location. A busy valet entrance is not automatically a workable tow-truck staging area. Confirm the plan before dispatching, then update the operator if the car or meeting point changes. Ask separately about any valet charges or outstanding arrangements.
