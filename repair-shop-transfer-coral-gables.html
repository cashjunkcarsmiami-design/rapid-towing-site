Moving a Vehicle Between Repair Shops in Coral Gables

September 28, 2026

Moving a car between repair facilities involves two separate handoffs. Before arranging transport, verify that the current shop can release the vehicle and that the next shop is ready to receive it.

Clarify release with the current shop

Ask who handles vehicle release, where the keys will be and whether the vehicle is ready for transport. Resolve any shop-specific paperwork or outstanding arrangements directly with that business. A towing reservation alone does not settle matters between you and the repair facility.

Describe the vehicle as it is now

Tell dispatch if the shop has removed components or if the vehicle's condition differs from when it arrived. Ask the shop for an accurate description when you are unsure. Do not assume a car under repair is ready to move simply because it previously drove into the facility.

Coordinate the receiving appointment

Obtain the next shop's address and receiving contact, then compare its intake arrangements with the proposed pickup. Confirm which items or keys accompany the car. Keep repair authorizations separate from the transport request so neither shop mistakes a tow for approval of additional work.

Resumen en español

Antes de mover un carro entre talleres, confirma que uno lo entrega y el otro lo recibe. Describe su condición actual y separa la autorización de reparación del traslado.
