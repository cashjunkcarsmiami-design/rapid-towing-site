Organizing a Fleet Towing Request in Doral

September 21, 2026

Use one company contact to coordinate a disabled fleet vehicle in Doral, from vehicle identification to destination approval and receipt details.

Identify the exact company vehicle

Fleet descriptions such as “the white van” can be ambiguous. Give dispatch the make, model and company unit identifier, plus the pickup address. Describe attached equipment or cargo that could affect the request. Ask whether the available equipment is suitable rather than assuming every company vehicle fits the same towing arrangement.

Choose one person to approve changes

Decide who can confirm the destination and authorize the quoted service. The employee at the vehicle, office manager and maintenance contact may all have useful information, but conflicting instructions slow the conversation. Share the primary contact and a backup number. If an employee must leave, agree on access and handoff arrangements with dispatch first.

Prepare the receiving and billing details

Confirm the receiving shop’s availability and provide the business name that should appear on the receipt. Ask which information is actually needed for billing instead of sending unrelated company records. Keep the tow reference with the vehicle’s maintenance record. If the destination changes, have the designated contact approve the revised transport details.
