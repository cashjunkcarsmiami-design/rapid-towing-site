When Another Vehicle Blocks a Scheduled Pickup in Hialeah

September 28, 2026

A scheduled pickup may look straightforward until another car blocks the space. Tell dispatch about that situation before arrival so the access problem can be discussed with the person who controls the parked vehicle.

Describe what is blocked

Explain where your vehicle sits and how the other car affects the approach. Distinguish between an occupied neighboring space and a vehicle directly blocking access. If you have a safe existing photo, ask whether it would help; do not step into traffic to obtain one.

Contact the right person

Ask the owner or property contact about moving the other vehicle. Do not promise that it will be gone until that arrangement is confirmed. A towing request for your car does not authorize moving someone else's vehicle, and access discussions should reflect that distinction.

Update the pickup plan

Let dispatch know when access is actually available and whether there is a limited window. Ask if the proposed pickup still works with the changed situation. If the obstruction remains, request guidance about rescheduling or assessment rather than trying to reposition a disabled car yourself.

Resumen en español

Si otro carro bloquea la recogida en Hialeah, avisa antes de la llegada. Coordina con su dueño o encargado y confirma el acceso; no autorices mover vehículos ajenos.
