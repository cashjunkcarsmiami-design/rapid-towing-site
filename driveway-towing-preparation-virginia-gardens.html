Before confirming your Virginia Gardens pickup

  • Show which driveway opening reaches the vehicle.

Confirm the service scope, destination, price and current availability directly with Rapid Towing at (305) 457-0817.

Describe the Vehicle Position

Explain whether the car faces the street or the house, whether another vehicle blocks it and whether the driveway is narrow, curved or steep.

Check Movement Details

Report whether the vehicle starts, shifts into neutral, rolls, steers and has keys. Mention flat tires, collision damage, locked wheels or parts touching the ground.

Remove Movable Obstacles

If safe and permitted, move other vehicles, unlock gates and remove loose items. Do not push an unstable car or work underneath it.

Confirm the Destination

Provide the repair shop or destination address and verify who can receive the vehicle. Stay reachable during the pickup window.
