Obtain instructions before the shop closes

Contact the intended shop while someone can answer questions. Ask whether it accepts after-hours deliveries and exactly where a towed vehicle may be left. Do not assume an open parking lot means unattended drop-off is allowed. Keep the name or role of the person who confirms the arrangement.

Coordinate keys and receiving confirmation

Ask how the shop wants keys handled and what information should accompany them. Relay only the necessary instructions to dispatch. Do not improvise a hiding place if the agreed method is unavailable. Confirm how the shop will acknowledge receipt when it reopens and which contact it should use if it has questions.

Agree on an alternative if access fails

Discuss what to do if the gate is closed or the designated area cannot be used. A different destination should be agreed with the towing operator, including any effect on the service charge. Do not treat an estimated arrival as a guarantee of reaching closing time. Keep both the customer and receiving contacts available.
