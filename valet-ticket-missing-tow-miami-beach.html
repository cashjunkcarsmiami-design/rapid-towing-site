Arranging a Valet Pickup Without the Claim Ticket in Miami Beach

October 19, 2026

If your vehicle needs towing from valet parking in Miami Beach and you cannot find the claim ticket, contact the valet desk first. The desk may need to confirm the vehicle and your authority to collect it. Sending a truck does not replace that release process.

Ask what the valet needs

Give the valet your vehicle description and ask which identification or booking details it requires. Follow the property’s process rather than assuming a towing request is enough. Confirm where the car is actually parked, especially if the storage area is different from the hotel entrance.

Tell dispatch when release is ready

Once release is arranged, share the valet contact and approved pickup point. Explain if the car cannot be brought out by valet staff. Do not schedule loading at a busy entrance unless the property and towing operator have confirmed a suitable plan.
