A Neighbor's Landmark Is Not the Pickup Entrance

Rapid Towing · October 25, 2026

A recognizable nearby business or property can help explain a general area, but it may not be the place where the vehicle is located. When requesting towing in Homestead, identify a landmark as a reference only. Do not allow it to replace the actual pickup address or authorized entrance.

Describe the relationship clearly

Explain whether the vehicle is before, beyond or across from the reference using information you can confirm. Avoid giving directions through someone else's property. If you are not at the vehicle, state where your information comes from and ask the current property contact to verify access. Do not invent distances to make the description sound precise.

Confirm the final location separately

Give dispatch the vehicle position and the contact who can meet the operator. A map pin on the landmark should not be treated as a pin on the car. If you notice that a message or screenshot uses the wrong place, correct it explicitly before the service plan is finalized. Accurate separation of reference point and destination reduces unnecessary searching without requiring you to move the disabled vehicle.
