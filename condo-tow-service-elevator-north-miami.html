A Service Elevator Booking Is Separate from Vehicle Access

Rapid Towing · October 26, 2026

A North Miami building may use a service elevator schedule for moving belongings, while a tow requires access to the vehicle area. If someone tells you to reserve an elevator, clarify what that reservation covers. It should not be treated as confirmation that a truck can reach the car.

Ask about the vehicle route specifically

Explain where the car is parked and ask the building contact which access arrangements apply to a pickup. Share any confirmed garage restrictions with dispatch. If you also need an elevator to bring down keys or personal items, keep that task distinct from the vehicle access question.

Coordinate both parts of the visit

Confirm who will meet the driver and how that person will reach the car. Tell dispatch if a building procedure could affect your availability at the agreed time. A clear plan connects the people, keys and vehicle without assuming that permission for one building facility automatically covers another.
