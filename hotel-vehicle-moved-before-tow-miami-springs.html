When a Hotel Moves Your Car Before a Tow in Miami Springs

October 22, 2026

Hotel staff may move a vehicle to another parking area after a towing request has been discussed. If your car is at a Miami Springs hotel, confirm its current position before sending final pickup instructions. A room number or the hotel's main entrance may not identify where the car actually is.

Check the location with the responsible staff

Ask which parking area now holds the car and who has the keys. Find out whether the access route differs from the guest entrance. If the vehicle no longer starts or cannot be moved normally, explain that condition rather than assuming staff can bring it to the front. Share only the hotel contact information needed for coordination.

Send one updated set of instructions

Notify dispatch when the vehicle moves and ask whether the new access affects the plan. Identify the person who can meet the driver or authorize access. Keep your checkout and personal transportation arrangements separate from the tow. A pickup confirmation does not automatically include waiting for luggage, transporting guests or retrieving keys elsewhere.
