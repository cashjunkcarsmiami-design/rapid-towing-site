Vehicle Dimensions to Discuss Before Towing in Medley

September 21, 2026

Describe a work vehicle and attached equipment clearly when requesting towing in Medley. Confirm suitability before a truck is assigned.

Describe the vehicle as it is configured

Tell dispatch the make, model and body type, including any box body, racks or permanent accessories. A modified work vehicle may differ from a standard passenger model. If you have reliable dimensions or weight information, say where it comes from. Avoid inventing measurements or treating a model name as a complete description.

Explain attachments and the current load

Mention trailers, equipment and cargo when discussing the job. Ask whether they can remain as configured or require separate arrangements. Do not assume a loaded vehicle and its trailer can be handled together. The operator needs to assess the request and confirm equipment suitability before promising transport.

Discuss access at both ends

Describe the entrance, maneuvering space and receiving area as well as the vehicle. A suitable truck still needs a workable pickup and delivery plan. Arrange a site contact who can answer questions and confirm the receiving location is ready. If configuration or loading changes after the quote, update dispatch before arrival.

Resumen en español

Describe carrocería, equipos añadidos, carga y acceso. El operador debe confirmar que puede atender esa configuración antes de asignar el servicio.
