A Cropped Screenshot of a Tow Estimate Can Miss Conditions

Rapid Towing · October 25, 2026

A screenshot that shows only a price may leave out the service scope or assumptions behind a towing estimate. When comparing or reviewing a request in Hallandale Beach, keep the surrounding explanation available. A number detached from its conditions can be misleading even when copied accurately.

Check the full proposal

Review which vehicle, pickup location and destination the estimate describes. Ask whether any details still require assessment and whether the screenshot omitted an important sentence. If you share the information with the person paying, protect unrelated personal information without removing the terms they need to understand. Do not present an old amount as current after changing the requested service.

Request a complete confirmation

Before authorizing work, ask dispatch to summarize the current scope and estimate together. Correct any vehicle or location mismatch and clarify what has not been confirmed. Keep that response with the request rather than relying on multiple cropped images. The purpose is to understand the actual proposed service, not to select a price without knowing which circumstances it covers.
