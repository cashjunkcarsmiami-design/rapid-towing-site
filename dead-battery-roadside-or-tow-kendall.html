Dead Battery in Kendall: Jump Start or Tow?

A silent vehicle may have a discharged battery, but electrical, starter, charging or mechanical problems can produce similar symptoms.

Report what happened before the car stopped

Tell dispatch whether lights were left on, warning lights appeared, the engine stalled while driving or repeated clicking occurs. Mention smoke, burning odor, damaged cables or flood exposure.

Avoid unsafe jump attempts

Do not jump a cracked, leaking, frozen or visibly damaged battery. Keep sparks away and follow vehicle-specific guidance. Hybrid and electric vehicles require appropriate procedures.

Know when transport may be needed

If a safe jump does not restore normal operation, the vehicle stalls again or serious warnings remain, driving may not be appropriate. Share the repair destination and whether the car can roll and steer.
