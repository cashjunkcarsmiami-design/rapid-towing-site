Towing in Hialeah: Picking Up a Used Car You Just Bought

Rapid Towing · November 2, 2026

Leer en español

Buying a non-running used car and arranging its transport are separate steps. Prepare a clear pickup agreement with the seller before requesting a tow in Hialeah.

Agree on who can release the car

Before booking towing, ask the seller who will be present and who is authorized to release the vehicle. Confirm that your purchase arrangements are settled separately; the towing operator is not acting as an escrow service or judging the sale. Tell dispatch whether the pickup is at a residence, dealer or repair property. If the seller will not attend, explain the proposed handoff and ask what authorization details are needed.

Check current facts instead of relying on the listing

An online description may be incomplete or out of date. Ask for the exact year, make and model, key availability and known movement limitations. Explain if the vehicle is missing wheels, has collision damage or has been partly dismantled. Do not promise dispatch that a car runs because the listing once said it did. Mark anything you have not confirmed as unknown so the operator can assess the request honestly.

Identify the usable Hialeah pickup entrance

A street address does not describe the route to a car parked behind a gate or inside a shared lot. Ask the seller for the authorized vehicle entrance and the contact who can open it. If another car blocks the exit, resolve that access question before dispatch relies on an arrival plan. Describe the actual parking surface and visible restrictions. Do not arrange to meet in a traffic lane for convenience.

Choose a destination that can receive the vehicle

Confirm whether the car is going to your property or directly to a repair business. Ask the receiving contact about permitted access and delivery timing. A disabled vehicle needs an agreed unloading location, not just the nearest street to an address. Tell dispatch about gates, narrow turns or a covered parking space at the destination too. If the destination changes after the quote, ask for an updated plan and price.

Keep the purchase price separate from towing

Ask for a quote for the actual vehicle and trip, then clarify what is included and how payment for transport will be handled. The vehicle purchase price does not establish a towing rate. Do not assume the seller has paid for collection unless dispatch confirms that arrangement. Keep sale documents and payment information out of public comments. Share only the release information requested through the agreed private channel.

Confirm the handoff before relying on a truck

Give Rapid Towing one reachable buyer contact and the seller or property contact for release. Confirm availability, equipment review, pickup window and destination before considering the request accepted. Let dispatch know promptly if the sale falls through or the vehicle moves. Call 305-457-0817 for towing in Hialeah and describe the car you actually bought; a website inquiry alone does not assign a truck.

What if the seller hands over a different key or vehicle?

Match the car waiting at the Hialeah pickup point to the vehicle details used for the quote before the handoff. If the seller reports a different model, missing key or new damage, notify dispatch and ask for reassessment. Do not describe an untested key as working. Resolve any disagreement about the purchase with the seller separately, and ask whether the tow remains available under the corrected facts. Transport arrangements do not establish ownership or validate a sales listing.
