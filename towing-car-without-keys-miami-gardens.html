Towing a Car Without Keys in Miami Gardens

A missing or damaged key does not describe the whole towing situation. Vehicle design, wheel position, parking access and authorization all affect how the request should be evaluated.

Start with authorization

Be ready to show that you are authorized to move the vehicle. Requirements depend on the circumstances and property, so confirm acceptable documentation with dispatch. A towing company cannot help bypass ownership or property rules.

Describe the car and parking position

Provide the year, make, model, drivetrain if known and exact parking location. Mention whether steering is locked, the parking brake is engaged, wheels are turned, tires are flat or the transmission cannot shift to neutral. Note tight spaces, gates, low roofs and cars parked close by.

Protect the handoff

Confirm the receiving shop, dealership or residence can accept the car without a working key. Tell the destination contact that unloading and placement may require instructions. Do not attempt to force the steering or transmission mechanism.
