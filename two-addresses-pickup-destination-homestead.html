Keeping Pickup and Delivery Addresses Clear for a Homestead Tow

October 22, 2026

When a vehicle in Homestead needs to go elsewhere, a conversation may include several addresses. Label the vehicle's current location and the requested destination separately. This is especially useful when the person arranging the service is calling from a third location.

State where the vehicle is now

Do not use your home address if the car is at another property. Give the actual pickup location and explain access details such as a gate, long driveway or position behind a building. Share the current condition and whether keys are available. If you do not know the precise location, resolve that uncertainty before treating an estimate as final.

Confirm who will receive the vehicle

Provide the destination name and address and verify that it can accept delivery. Ask whether the proposed timing works for that location. If you change the destination, notify dispatch and request an updated estimate or confirmation. A new address can change the trip; it should not be passed only to someone waiting at pickup.
