Correcting a Pickup Pin That Points to the Wrong Side in West Miami

October 22, 2026

A map pin can identify the correct property while pointing toward a wall, rear alley or entrance that vehicles cannot use. For a tow in West Miami, pair the address with clear access instructions. The goal is to show how to reach the vehicle, not just where the property appears on a map.

Explain the difference between address and entrance

Name the street used for entry and describe a visible landmark without sharing unnecessary private details. If you send a pin, say whether it marks the vehicle or the authorized entrance. Do not place yourself in traffic to take a reference photo. Mention one-way access or other restrictions you actually know rather than guessing a route for the driver.

Replace conflicting directions

If an earlier message pointed to another entrance, explicitly correct it. Confirm a contact who can answer questions during the agreed window. Notify dispatch if a gate closes or the vehicle moves. Keep the destination separately labeled so the pickup pin is not confused with the repair shop or drop-off address.
