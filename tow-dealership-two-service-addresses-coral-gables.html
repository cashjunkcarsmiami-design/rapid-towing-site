Two Service Addresses for One Dealership in Coral Gables

Rapid Towing · October 24, 2026

A dealership can list more than one address for sales, service or vehicle storage. Before arranging a tow to a Coral Gables destination, confirm the receiving location directly with the service contact. A familiar business name does not identify the entrance where your vehicle should be delivered.

Ask for the exact receiving instructions

Request the address, relevant entrance and contact who will accept the vehicle. If an appointment message shows a different location, ask which instruction is current. Do not assume a sales lot can receive a disabled vehicle outside its normal intake process. Keep any appointment reference with the confirmed destination rather than using it as a substitute for an address.

Pass the confirmed location to dispatch

Explain if an earlier destination must be replaced and ask whether the change affects the estimate or coordination. Confirm who will handle the keys and any receiving requirements. If the dealership has not confirmed acceptance, tell dispatch instead of treating the destination as settled. One complete receiving instruction is more useful than several business addresses that leave the operator to choose.
