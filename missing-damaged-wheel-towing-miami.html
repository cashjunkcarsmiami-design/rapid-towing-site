Towing a Car With a Missing or Damaged Wheel in Miami

October 29, 2026 · Rapid Towing

Tell dispatch about a missing wheel, damaged tire or displaced wheel before a Miami towing request. Confirm equipment and access for the actual vehicle.

State exactly what is missing or damaged

A flat tire, an absent wheel and a wheel pushed out of position are not the same condition. When asking Rapid Towing about a pickup in Miami or Doral, identify the affected corner of the vehicle and describe what you can see. Explain whether the tire remains on the rim and whether the wheel is attached. Do not diagnose suspension or steering damage from a photograph if it has not been professionally assessed.

Explain how the vehicle is currently supported

Tell dispatch if the car is resting on a jack, stands, the ground or another support. Do not crawl underneath, remove supports or attempt to roll it for the pickup. If the car appears unstable, keep people away from its potential movement area and describe the concern. A photo from a safe distance can help the operator assess the request without requiring you to touch a damaged component.

Describe access as well as the damaged corner

Explain the parking surface, slope, nearby walls, gate opening and whether there is room around the affected side. At a Hialeah Gardens business, coordinate access with the person authorized to open the property. A car beside a wall or inside a low garage may require a different review from one in an open lot. Do not assume that choosing “flatbed” by itself resolves every loading restriction.

Ask dispatch to assess equipment before the visit

Share the year, make, model, photos and known condition before requesting confirmation. Ask whether the company can handle that specific vehicle and what information is still needed. Equipment and loading arrangements must be confirmed for the actual job; a website photograph is an example of previous work, not a guarantee of a particular setup. Leave loading, attachment points and vehicle movement to the operator.

Confirm the receiving location

Tell the repair shop about the wheel condition before arranging delivery. Confirm that it can receive the vehicle, the correct entrance and the person expecting it. Ask dispatch to confirm the destination, service scope, price and arrival estimate. Report any change before pickup, such as a wheel being removed after you sent the first photos.
