A Dashboard Message Disappeared Before Your Kendall Tow Request

Rapid Towing · October 23, 2026

A dashboard message may disappear after the vehicle is switched off. When requesting a tow in Kendall, describe what you observed without assuming the underlying condition has been resolved just because the message is no longer visible.

Report the observation and timing

If you already have a photograph, explain when it was taken. Otherwise, describe the wording as accurately as you remember and mark anything uncertain. Do not restart or drive the vehicle merely to reproduce the message. State any current symptoms you can observe without additional tests.

Ask about transport for the present condition

Give dispatch the vehicle details and explain whether it has moved since the message appeared. A remembered warning is useful context, but it is not a diagnosis or proof of suitability for a particular towing method. If a technician provides new information, relay the relevant facts before confirming equipment. Keep the proposed destination separate from any assumption about the repair needed.
