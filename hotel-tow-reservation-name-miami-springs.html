A Hotel Reservation Under Another Name Before a Miami Springs Tow

Rapid Towing · October 24, 2026

A hotel may identify your parking arrangement using a name different from the person requesting towing. If you are coordinating a pickup in Miami Springs, clarify that with the property before dispatch arrives. A reservation name is an access reference, not proof that the person calling can authorize every action involving the vehicle.

Ask the property what reference it needs

Use the hotel's normal contact channel to confirm who can release access and what details should be supplied. Do not send full reservation confirmations or personal identification publicly. Give dispatch the relevant property contact and vehicle location after those arrangements are understood. Keep the vehicle owner's authorization separate from the parking discussion.

Prepare the handoff before departure

If the guest named on the reservation will leave first, ask how that affects access and key handling. Do not assume a front desk will accept keys or supervise a pickup without agreeing to it. Confirm a practical contact who will still be available when needed. Changes in the hotel arrangement should be communicated to dispatch before the previously discussed pickup plan is treated as final.
