Handling Parking Credentials During a North Miami Tow

October 19, 2026

A vehicle in a residential garage may use a parking decal, access card or transponder. When arranging a North Miami tow, separate the vehicle key from building access credentials. Property access rules can differ, so ask the building contact how the tow operator should enter and leave.

Identify what must stay with you

Ask the property whether a permit remains with the vehicle or must be retained by the resident. Tell dispatch if entry requires an escort instead of a reusable card. Avoid leaving your only building access device in a car that is headed to a repair shop without a plan to get it back.

Confirm exit arrangements too

An entrance that opens for a resident may not describe the truck exit route. Relay any directions provided by management and identify the person who can assist onsite. Never assume towing authorization gives the driver permission to use every resident-only access point.
