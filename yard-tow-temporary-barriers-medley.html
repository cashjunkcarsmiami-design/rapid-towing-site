Temporary Barriers Inside a Medley Yard Before a Tow

Rapid Towing · October 24, 2026

An entrance can be open while temporary barriers inside a commercial yard prevent access to the vehicle. When requesting a tow in Medley, describe the route beyond the gate as well as the gate itself. A photograph of the street entrance does not explain the internal access conditions.

Check with the site contact

Ask the responsible person whether barriers, pallets or temporary work areas affect the planned pickup. Do not move another business's equipment or promise that it will be cleared without authorization. Tell dispatch what is known and which conditions remain uncertain. If photos are permitted, take them from a safe location without entering active work zones.

Coordinate before confirming access

Identify who can answer questions when the operator arrives and whether any site instructions apply. A clear route described earlier may change during the workday, so report new obstructions promptly. Do not assume a towing vehicle can follow the same route as a passenger car. Dispatch must assess suitability using the actual vehicle and site conditions before the service arrangement is finalized.
