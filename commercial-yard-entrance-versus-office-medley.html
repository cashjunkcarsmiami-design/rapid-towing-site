Choosing the Correct Yard Entrance for a Tow in Medley

October 22, 2026

A business address in Medley can lead to an office door rather than the yard holding the vehicle. Before arranging towing, identify the entrance that the property authorizes for collection. Do not assume a truck can reach the car through the same route used by visitors on foot.

Describe the authorized route

Ask the site contact which gate should be used and whether there are check-in instructions or operating-hour limits. Mention tight turns, overhead restrictions and active loading areas. Share a current reference photo if it can be taken safely. Do not estimate that equipment will fit; give dispatch the conditions so suitability can be assessed.

Confirm access close to the pickup window

Check who will be available at the gate and whether yard activity might block the route. If the entrance changes, notify dispatch before arrival. Keep private access codes in direct communications, not public posts. Confirm the vehicle and destination separately: permission to enter a yard does not establish which unit should be collected or where it should be delivered.
