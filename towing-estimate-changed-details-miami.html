Why a Towing Estimate May Need Updating in Miami

October 19, 2026

A towing estimate is based on the information available when you call. A different destination, a car that cannot roll or an entrance that will not accommodate the planned truck can change the job. Before approving transport in Miami, ask which assumptions the estimate includes.

Separate distance from loading conditions

Explain both addresses and describe where the vehicle sits. A short trip can still involve difficult access. Tell dispatch about missing keys, blocked wheels or a covered parking area before requesting a final scope. Do not treat a price for a straightforward pickup as confirmation that every recovery situation is included.

Confirm changes before work proceeds

If something changes, contact dispatch and ask for the revised scope and total before authorizing additional work. Keep the updated message with the original estimate. Ask whether waiting time, access equipment or destination changes affect the price rather than assuming a particular fee applies.
