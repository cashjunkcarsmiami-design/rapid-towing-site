Making Passenger Plans During a Tow in Miami Gardens

September 28, 2026

Transporting a disabled vehicle and getting its occupants to their destination are two different needs. Discuss both early so your group does not discover an unconfirmed assumption when the truck arrives.

Tell dispatch about the occupants

State how many people are with the car and ask what, if any, passenger arrangement can be offered. Do not assume there is seating for everyone or that special passenger needs can be accommodated. Let the operator give a definite answer for the actual service.

Coordinate a separate ride when needed

Agree on where and how your group will travel once you know the transport arrangement. Keep communication with dispatch open while making those plans. If there is an immediate emergency, contact emergency services; a routine towing inquiry does not replace emergency assistance.

Keep the vehicle handoff covered

If occupants leave separately, confirm who will remain responsible for release and the keys. Keep essential belongings with the people who need them when they can be retrieved safely. Tell dispatch if the on-site contact changes so arranging passenger travel does not interrupt the agreed pickup.

Resumen en español

Pregunta por separado cómo viajarán los ocupantes. No supongas que caben todos en la grúa y confirma quién entregará el carro si alguien se marcha.
