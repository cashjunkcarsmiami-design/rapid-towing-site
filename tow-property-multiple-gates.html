Several Gates on One Property: Planning a Tow in Homestead

Rapid Towing · October 23, 2026

A property with several gates may have only one suitable route to the vehicle. Before a planned tow in Homestead, identify the entrance the property contact actually authorizes and describe where it leads.

Explain each relevant distinction

State whether the entrance used by visitors differs from the vehicle access point. Use a visible description or a photo taken safely, and say if you do not know the clearance. Do not direct the operator across a neighboring property or through a gate whose access has not been confirmed.

Verify the route is available that day

Ask the responsible person whether equipment, parked vehicles or scheduled work will affect the route. Update dispatch if the access changes after the initial assessment. Being able to open a gate does not guarantee that the truck can reach or load the vehicle. Confirm the actual pickup conditions and who will be present before treating the arrangement as ready.

Resumen en español

Identifica el portón autorizado y comprueba el recorrido hasta el vehículo. Abrir la entrada no garantiza que el equipo pueda realizar la recogida.
