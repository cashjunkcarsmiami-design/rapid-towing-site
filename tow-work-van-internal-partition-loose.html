A Loose Interior Partition in a Work Van Awaiting Towing

Rapid Towing · October 24, 2026

A work van's interior partition may look installed while some of its attachments are loose. When requesting towing in Hialeah Gardens, disclose that condition along with the vehicle details. Do not assume that a feature inside the van is irrelevant to transport planning.

Describe without testing the installation

Explain what you observed and whether any parts have already been removed. Do not push, pull or lean on the partition to demonstrate the problem. If a safe photograph is possible, show its position and describe any unknowns. Mention nearby cargo separately, especially if the van still contains tools or other business equipment.

Ask what must be addressed before service

Dispatch needs to assess the disclosed condition and confirm whether additional preparation is required. Do not assume that securing or removing interior equipment is included in the towing request. Have the responsible business contact clarify what can be prepared appropriately and when. If the condition changes before pickup, update dispatch and reconfirm the plan rather than relying on an estimate based on a different vehicle setup.
