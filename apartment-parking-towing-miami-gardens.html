Planning a Tow From a Miami Gardens Apartment Lot

Apartment communities may restrict vendor entry, loading areas and service hours. Confirm the property process before the truck arrives.

Get the approved access instructions

Ask management or security about gate codes, vendor check-in, resident escort and permitted hours. Provide the building and parking-space number.

Describe tight parking conditions

Mention nearby cars, curbs, carports, low trees, flat tires and locked steering. State whether the vehicle can shift to neutral and whether keys are present.

Confirm destination and authorization

The person requesting transport should be authorized to move the vehicle. Give the receiving address and verify it can accept the car, especially after hours.
