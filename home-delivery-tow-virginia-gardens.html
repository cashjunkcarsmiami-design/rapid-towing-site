Receiving a Towed Vehicle at Home in Virginia Gardens

September 28, 2026

A home address identifies the destination but does not describe where a vehicle can be delivered. Discuss the available space with dispatch before assuming that any driveway or parking area will work.

Explain the proposed receiving space

Describe whether the destination is a driveway, assigned space or shared area. Mention any known restrictions and vehicles already occupying it. The driver needs to assess the actual arrangement; a street address alone cannot confirm unloading access or a suitable position.

Confirm who can receive the vehicle

Identify the homeowner or authorized contact and verify that they expect the delivery. If you are arranging it for someone else, make sure their agreement is clear before using their address. Ask how the key handoff and arrival communication will be handled.

Keep the space available

Coordinate any permitted space preparation with the property contact before arrival. If the proposed location becomes unavailable, inform dispatch instead of directing the driver to an unapproved area. Confirm the final placement with the operator; avoid treating the transport request as a promise that the car can be positioned anywhere you choose.

Resumen en español

Para recibir un carro en casa, describe el espacio y confirma quién lo recibirá. La dirección no garantiza que la grúa pueda descargar en cualquier lugar.
